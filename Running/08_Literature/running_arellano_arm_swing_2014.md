# Literature - The metabolic cost of human running: is swinging the arms worth it?

## Citation

PMID: 25031455. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25031455/

Authors recorded: Christopher J Arellano, Rodger Kram.

## Topic

arm swing and running

## Principal Relevant Finding

Experimental arm-restriction conditions changed whole-body rotational behaviour and metabolic demand, supporting analysis of arms within whole-body coordination.

## Limitation

Experimental restriction conditions do not define an ideal arm-swing amplitude for an individual runner.
