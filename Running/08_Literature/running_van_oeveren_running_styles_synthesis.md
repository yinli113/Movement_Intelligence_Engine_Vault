# Running biomechanics and running styles
van Oeveren, B. T., de Ruiter, C. J., Beek, P. J., & van Dieën, J. H. *The biomechanics of running and running styles: a synthesis*. Sports Biomechanics 23(4), 516–554 (2024; online 2021). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/14763141.2021.1873411); [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33663325/).

## Claim scope
The review discusses reduced/delayed swing-knee flexion and increased swing-leg moment of inertia, within relationships between propulsion timing and step frequency. This is general biomechanical synthesis, not a knee-flexion intervention trial. It does not validate TillYes angles or establish an athlete's hip-flexor effort, economy, or optimal knee flexion.
