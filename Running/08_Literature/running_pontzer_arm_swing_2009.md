# Literature - Control and function of arm swing in human walking and running

## Citation

PMID: 19181900. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19181900/

Authors recorded: Herman Pontzer, John H Holloway IV, David A Raichlen, Daniel E Lieberman.

## Topic

arm swing control

## Principal Relevant Finding

The experiment supported a passive mass-damper role for the arms that reduces torso/head rotation; restricting arm swing did not change locomotor cost in this study.

## Limitation

Ten healthy adults and specific treadmill conditions; the result does not establish individual optimal arm amplitude.
