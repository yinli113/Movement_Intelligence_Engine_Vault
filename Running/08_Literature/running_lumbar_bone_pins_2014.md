# Literature - Three-dimensional lumbar spine vertebral motion during running using indwelling bone pins

## Citation

PMID: 25341976. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25341976/

Authors recorded: MacWilliams BA, Rozumalski A, Swanson AN, Wervey R, Dykes DC, Novacheck TF, Schwartz MH.

## Topic

vertebral motion and surface-marker limitation

## Principal Relevant Finding

Direct vertebral measurement demonstrates that lumbar regions move during running and cautions against treating surface landmarks as direct skeletal motion.

## Limitation

Invasive measurement conditions and small specialised samples limit generalisation; it does not validate ordinary pose rotation.
