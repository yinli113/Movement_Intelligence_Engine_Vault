# Literature - Influence of Low Back Pain Status on Pelvis-Trunk Coordination During Walking and Running

## Citation

Seay JF, Van Emmerik RE, Hamill J. (2011). *Influence of low back pain status on pelvis-trunk coordination during walking and running.* Clinical Biomechanics, 26(6), 572-579. PMID: 21377771. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21377771/

## Topic

Thorax-pelvis transverse counter-rotation, relative phase dissociation, and "block rotation" pathology.

## Principal Relevant Findings

1. **Out-of-Phase Counter-Rotation ($12^\circ - 18^\circ$)**: Healthy, uninjured runners exhibit prominent out-of-phase transverse counter-rotation between the thorax and pelvis (averaging $12^\circ$ to $18^\circ$ of relative excursion), which stores helical elastic energy and neutralizes whole-body angular momentum.
2. **Block Rotation ($< 11^\circ$)**: Runners with back pain or restricted mobility demonstrate in-phase "block rotation," where the pelvis and thorax rotate together as a rigid cylinder with less than $10^\circ - 11^\circ$ dissociation.
3. **Mechanical Consequence**: In block rotation, the abdominal and helical oblique slings fail to absorb and reverse transverse momentum, transferring unmitigated torsional impact directly into the lumbar spine and lower limb joints.

## Limitation

Camera-derived visual angles describe orientation proxies. Reduced dissociation indicates a rotational coordination pattern requiring mobility screening, not a definitive spinal diagnosis.
