# Literature - The Biomechanics of Running

## Citation

Novacheck TF. (1998). *The biomechanics of running.* Gait & Posture, 7(1), 77-95. PMID: 10221387. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10221387/

## Topic

Fundamental running gait cycle kinematics, stance/flight phasing, and normative pelvic excursions.

## Principal Relevant Findings

1. **Normative Coronal Pelvic Oscillation**: During peak single-leg support (mid-stance), coronal pelvic list/tilt excursion in uninjured runners naturally remains bounded within a 3° to 5° range.
2. **Dynamic Balance of Center of Mass**: Pelvic motion in the coronal and transverse planes serves to minimize unnecessary vertical and horizontal center of mass excursion, conserving kinetic energy during stance transitions.

## Limitation

Normative ranges describe population medians in standard laboratory settings. They do not define an arbitrary failure threshold for individual runners who have developed safe functional adaptations.
