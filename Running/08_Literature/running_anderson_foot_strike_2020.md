# Literature - What are the Benefits and Risks Associated with Changing Foot Strike Pattern During Running? A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Injury, Running Economy, and Biomechanics

## Citation

PMID: 31823338. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31823338/

Authors recorded: Laura M Anderson, Daniel R Bonanno, Harvi F Hart, Christian J Barton.

## Topic

foot strike

## Principal Relevant Finding

Changing foot-strike pattern cannot be universally recommended for uninjured habitual rearfoot runners; strike patterns redistribute biomechanical demands rather than defining a universally superior technique.

## Limitation

The evidence base was heterogeneous and did not establish universal injury-prevention or economy benefits.
