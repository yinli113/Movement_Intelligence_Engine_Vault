# Literature - Stiffness and Running Performance: From the Tissue to the Body: A Narrative Review

## Citation

PMID: 41863688. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41863688/

Authors recorded: Lola Masson, Guillaume Y Millet, Hugo A Kerhervé.

## Topic

stiffness across measurement scales

## Principal Relevant Finding

Stiffness has multiple definitions and measurement scales; links with running economy remain mechanistically unclear and no universal optimal level is established.

## Limitation

Narrative review; tissue-level evidence remains underexplored and measurement methods are not interchangeable.
