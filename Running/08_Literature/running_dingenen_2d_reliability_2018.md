# Reliability of 2D running measurements
Dingenen et al. (2018), Physical Therapy in Sport 33:40–47. [DOI 10.1016/j.ptsp.2018.06.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ptsp.2018.06.009), [PMID 30005426](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30005426/). Primary abstract checked 2026-10-16.

## Findings — Level 3 reliability study
Twenty-one recreational runners were measured on two occasions. A mean of at least seven steps was recommended for the specific measured variables. Reliability differed by variable; pelvic drop was less reliable than some other measures.

## Correction and application
The prior “steps (or strides) per side” wording conflated sampling units. The paper does not validate a universal seven-stride threshold, a four-to-five-stride rejection threshold, shoulder/head phase lag, or MediaPipe tracking. A provisional app rule must be identified separately as Level 5.
