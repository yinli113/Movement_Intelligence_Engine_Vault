# Speed and spine–pelvis motion
Preece SJ, Mason D, Bramah C (2016). *How do elite endurance runners alter movements of the spine and pelvis as running speed increases?* [DOI 10.1016/j.gaitpost.2016.03.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gaitpost.2016.03.011); [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27131190/), checked 2026-10-16.

Study design: repeated-speed kinematics; observational elite/recreational group comparison, 14 participants per group. Both groups showed similar ROM adaptations, most pronounced transversely. Thorax inclination responses differed. Group membership was not randomized; performance benefits of adopting a posture were not tested.

Use speed as a comparison condition in [[running_pelvis_thorax_rotation]]; do not turn elite group characteristics into ideal posture rules. Related source: [[running_preece_spine_pelvis_2016]].
