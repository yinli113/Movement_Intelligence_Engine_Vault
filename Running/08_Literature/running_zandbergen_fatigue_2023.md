# Literature - Effects of level running-induced fatigue on running kinematics: A systematic review and meta-analysis

## Citation

PMID: 36332318. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36332318/

Authors recorded: Zandbergen MA, Marotta L, Bulthuis R, Buurke JH, Veltink PH, Reenalda J.

## Topic

fatigue and running kinematics

## Principal Relevant Finding

Fatigue-related kinematic changes vary and show substantial inter-individual differences, supporting subject-specific multi-stride analysis.

## Limitation

Protocols, runner experience, and measured outcomes vary; time trend alone does not prove fatigue.
