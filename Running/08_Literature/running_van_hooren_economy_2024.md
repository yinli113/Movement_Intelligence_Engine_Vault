# Literature - The Relationship Between Running Biomechanics and Running Economy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Observational Studies

## Topic

running biomechanics and economy

## Principal Relevant Finding

Individual biomechanical variables show limited associations with metabolic running economy; smaller vertical displacement had an association but cannot substitute for physiological measurement.

## Limitation

Observational associations do not support a camera-derived economy score or causal prescription.
