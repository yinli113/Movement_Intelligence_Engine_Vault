# Sprint coordination and frontal CoM displacement
Vellucci CL & Beaudette SM (2025), J Appl Biomech 41(6):561–570. [DOI 10.1123/jab.2024-0221](https://doi.org/10.1123/jab.2024-0221); [PMID 41139408](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41139408/). Primary abstract checked 2026-10-16.

## Observational/modelled association — Level 3
The study related maximal-sprint velocity and frontal CoM displacement to multijoint coordination. It supports investigating segment relationships rather than treating one segment independently.

## Corrected transfer boundary
The abstract does not directly demonstrate head-stabilization mechanisms or validate marathon shoulder/pelvis 2D tilt. A head–trunk–pelvis explanation remains a hypothesis. Pelvic midpoint is not whole-body CoM. Full methods review is still required for detailed sensor/model transfer.
