## Citation

Authors recorded: Allison G Schubert, James Kempf, Bryan C Heiderscheit.

## Topic

stride frequency and length

## Principal Relevant Finding

Manipulating stride frequency or length changes running mechanics, supporting joint interpretation of cadence, landing, and stride context.

## Limitation

Mechanical responses do not establish one universally optimal stride frequency.
