# Literature - Biomechanical Differences of Foot-Strike Patterns During Running: A Systematic Review With Meta-analysis

## Citation

PMID: 26304644. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26304644/

Authors recorded: Almeida MO, Davis IS, Lopes AD.

## Topic

foot-strike biomechanics

## Principal Relevant Finding

Foot-strike patterns differ in biomechanical demand distribution; the finding supports describing strategy rather than assigning a universal correctness score.

## Limitation

Biomechanical differences do not by themselves establish injury causation or an optimal strike pattern.
