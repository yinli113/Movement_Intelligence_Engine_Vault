# Literature - Vertical and Leg Stiffness Modeling During Running: Effect of Speed and Incline

## Citation

PMID: 36854390. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36854390/

Authors recorded: Meyer F, Falbriard M, Aminian K, Millet GP.

## Topic

leg and vertical stiffness modelling

## Principal Relevant Finding

Running stiffness is model-dependent and varies with task conditions; video may describe deformation but not stiffness without compatible force inputs.

## Limitation

Instrumented/modelled stiffness must not be equated with a camera-only leg-length ratio.
