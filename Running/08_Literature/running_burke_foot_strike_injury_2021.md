# Literature - Risk Factors for Injuries in Runners: A Systematic Review of Foot Strike Technique and Its Classification at Impact

## Citation

PMID: 34527750. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34527750/

Authors recorded: Burke A, Dillon S, O'Connor S, Whyte EF, Gore S, Moran KA.

## Topic

foot strike and injury

## Principal Relevant Finding

The review found low evidence for a relationship between foot-strike technique and running-related injury; included categorical evidence was often very low quality.

## Limitation

Only 13 studies were included; many were retrospective, small, or used weak strike-classification methods.
