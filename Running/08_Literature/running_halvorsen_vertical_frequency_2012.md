# Acute changes in vertical displacement and step frequency
Halvorsen K, Eriksson M, Gullstrand L (2012; PMID indexed ahead of print 2011). [DOI 10.1519/JSC.0b013e318239f87f](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e318239f87f); [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22027846/), checked 2026-10-16.

Experimental feedback study: 16 male runners at 16 km/h. Altering vertical displacement and step frequency involved interacting changes in metabolic cost and CoM height. This does not support a universal instruction to minimize visible bounce. Full protocol/effect extraction remains pending.

Reasoning: [[running_running_economy]] should compare intervention evidence with observational correlations before proposing any individual change. [[running_vertical_oscillation]] remains a camera proxy.
