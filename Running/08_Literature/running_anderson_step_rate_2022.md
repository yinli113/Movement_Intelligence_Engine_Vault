# Literature - What is the Effect of Changing Running Step Rate on Injury, Performance and Biomechanics? A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

## Citation

PMID: 36057913. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36057913/

Authors recorded: Laura M Anderson, Joel F Martin, Christian J Barton, Daniel R Bonanno.

## Topic

step rate

## Principal Relevant Finding

Increasing step rate changes multiple kinematic and loading-related variables, while evidence for injury and performance outcomes remains limited; changes should be contextual rather than tied to a universal cadence.

## Limitation

Injury and performance evidence was very limited and biomechanical protocols varied.
