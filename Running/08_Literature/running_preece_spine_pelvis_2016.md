# Spine–pelvis coordination

## Citation and verification
Preece, Stephen J.; Mason, Duncan; Bramah, Christopher (2016). Human Movement Science 45:110–118. [DOI 10.1016/j.humov.2015.11.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.humov.2015.11.014); [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26618444/), checked 2026-10-16. Abstract-level audit; not a new full-text extraction.

## Observational findings — Level 3
Kinematics from 28 runners supported sagittal/frontal antiphase coordination during stance and a transverse pelvis lag behind the thorax.

## Theoretical explanation in the paper
Managing CoM acceleration and driving arm motion were proposed rationales. These are not experimental demonstrations of head stabilization, fascia loading or energy storage. Bilateral shoulder landmarks are not equivalent to the study's thorax segment definition.

## Source separation
The speed/group paper is distinct: [[running_preece_speed_2016]]. The earlier combined note incorrectly claimed greater range-of-motion adaptation in elite runners. The speed paper reports similar ROM changes between groups.
