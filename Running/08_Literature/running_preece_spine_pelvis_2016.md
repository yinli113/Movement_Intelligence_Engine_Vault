# Literature - The coordinated movement of the spine and pelvis during running

## Citation

PMID: 26618444. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26618444/

Authors recorded: Stephen J Preece.

## Topic

spine-pelvis coordination

## Principal Relevant Finding

The study supports treating pelvis and spinal/trunk segments as coordinated rather than one rigid unit during running.

## Limitation

Instrumented segment coordination does not validate monocular pose estimates of true 3D vertebral rotation.
