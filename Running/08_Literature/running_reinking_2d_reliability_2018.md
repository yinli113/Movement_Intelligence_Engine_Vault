# Reliability of manual 2D running analysis

Reinking MF, Dugan L, Ripple N, Schleper K, Scholz H, Spadino J, Stahl C, McPoil TG (2018). International Journal of Sports Physical Therapy **13(3):453–461**. [PMC6044590](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6044590/), PMID 30038831. The previous author list and 13(4):653–661 citation were incorrect.

Six raters analysed ten runners. Frontal intra-rater reliability varied substantially; frontal inter-rater reliability was generally high. Frontal variables were hip adduction and rearfoot angles at contact/midstance, not shoulder tilt or pelvic roll. Findings do not establish automated landmark validity or quantify camera yaw effects. General projection limitations remain separate geometric considerations.
