# Literature - Effects of running-induced fatigue on joint kinematics and kinetics during overground running: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Authors recorded: Dylan Trowell, Claire Kenneally-Dabrowski, Jason Bonacci.

## Topic

overground fatigue

## Principal Relevant Finding

The synthesis supports increased ground-contact time with fatigue at consistent speeds while many joint variables may show smaller or inconsistent changes.

## Limitation

The app must distinguish an observed early-late trend from independently established physiological fatigue.
