# Recovery-knee flexion and swing-leg inertia

Observe the time course of projected knee flexion and thigh motion in side view. Label it recovery/swing only with adequate phase evidence. Knee folding may alter mass distribution about the hip; no effort or economy conclusion follows from the image angle alone.

Required TillYes inputs: `running.knee.flexion`, `running.swing.thigh`; measured cadence and speed context for cadence comparisons. Segment mass distribution and 3D kinematics would be needed to estimate inertia. No numerical optimum is established.

Ask whether knee-flexion timing and cadence vary together at matched speed. Alternatives include speed, limb proportions, propulsion timing, camera projection and tracking.
