# Running - Deep Front Line

## Definition

A running-domain hypothesis node relating supported whole-chain compression/support patterns to the existing Deep Front Line framework.

## Why the Line Is Considered

The [[deep_front_line]] provides a deep longitudinal continuity model; pose video cannot detect deep tissue behaviour.

## Required Biomechanical Interpretation

- [[running_different_load_distribution_strategy]]
- [[running_compression_and_rebound]]

## Hypothesis

Repeated compression/support differences may make Deep Front Line relationships candidates only after biomechanical interpretation and clinical context.

## Evidence Gate

Require a repeated Level B pattern, a supported Level C interpretation, sufficient measurement confidence, and explicit hypothesis wording under [[running_evidence_gate]].

## Direct Assessment / Retest

Repeat the movement trial and assess breathing, medial foot/leg, hip, trunk, and relevant symptoms directly as clinically appropriate.

## Camera Boundary

No camera view measures fascial force, tension, stiffness, contribution, restriction, or muscle activation. Camera view requirements belong to the upstream measurements and interpretations.

## Permitted Reporting Language

"This repeated movement pattern may support a Deep Front Line hypothesis requiring clinical correlation."

## Forbidden Claims

- "Deep Front Line is tight."
- "Deep Front Line dysfunction was detected."
- Any treatment prescription derived from video alone.
