# Running - Spiral Line

## Definition

A running-domain hypothesis node that links rotational and foot-to-trunk patterns to the existing Spiral Line framework.

## Why the Line Is Considered

The existing [[spiral_line]] provides a diagonal foot-leg-pelvis-trunk-scapula-neck anatomy model; running video only supplies movement proxies.

## Required Biomechanical Interpretation

- [[running_cross_body_myofascial_hypothesis]]
- [[running_altered_rotational_coordination]]

## Hypothesis

Persistent rotational coupling, foot-orientation, and cross-body timing differences may make the Spiral Line a candidate clinical reasoning pathway.

## Evidence Gate

Require a repeated Level B pattern, a supported Level C interpretation, sufficient measurement confidence, and explicit hypothesis wording under [[running_evidence_gate]].

## Direct Assessment / Retest

Repeat front/back trials, assess the relevant joints and cross-body rotation directly, and compare symptom response without assuming tightness.

## Camera Boundary

No camera view measures fascial force, tension, stiffness, contribution, restriction, or muscle activation. Camera view requirements belong to the upstream measurements and interpretations.

## Permitted Reporting Language

"This repeated movement pattern may support a Spiral Line hypothesis requiring clinical correlation."

## Forbidden Claims

- "Spiral Line is tight."
- "Spiral Line dysfunction was detected."
- Any treatment prescription derived from video alone.
