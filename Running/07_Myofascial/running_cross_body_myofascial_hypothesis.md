# Running - Cross Body Myofascial Hypothesis

## Definition

A hypothesis that a repeated cross-body coordination difference may be considered alongside diagonal myofascial relationships.

## Why the Line Is Considered

It is the explicit D-level bridge from rotational biomechanics to line candidates.

## Required Biomechanical Interpretation

- [[running_altered_rotational_coordination]]
- [[running_cross_body_coordination]]
- [[running_variability]]

## Hypothesis

The repeated cross-body difference may be compatible with altered contribution through Spiral or Functional Line relationships.

## Evidence Gate

Require a repeated Level B pattern, a supported Level C interpretation, sufficient measurement confidence, and explicit hypothesis wording under [[running_evidence_gate]].

## Direct Assessment / Retest

Recheck across matched-speed trials, compare arm-leg and pelvis-thorax timing, then use clinical rotational and cross-body loading tests.

## Camera Boundary

No camera view measures fascial force, tension, stiffness, contribution, restriction, or muscle activation. Camera view requirements belong to the upstream measurements and interpretations.

## Permitted Reporting Language

"This repeated movement pattern may support a Cross Body Myofascial Hypothesis hypothesis requiring clinical correlation."

## Forbidden Claims

- "Cross Body Myofascial Hypothesis is tight."
- "Cross Body Myofascial Hypothesis dysfunction was detected."
- Any treatment prescription derived from video alone.
