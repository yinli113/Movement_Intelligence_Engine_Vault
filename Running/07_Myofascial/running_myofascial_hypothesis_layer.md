# Running - Myofascial Hypothesis Layer

## Definition

The final, optional interpretation layer that maps supported biomechanical interpretations to existing Anatomy Trains line concepts without claiming tissue state.

## Why the Line Is Considered

It prevents a shortcut from pose geometry to fascial diagnosis.

## Required Biomechanical Interpretation

- [[running_altered_rotational_coordination]]
- [[running_longer_landing_strategy]]
- [[running_different_load_distribution_strategy]]

## Hypothesis

A repeated, supported movement interpretation may justify naming one or more candidate line relationships for clinical correlation.

## Candidate Line Hypotheses

- [[running_spiral_line]]
- [[running_functional_lines]]
- [[running_lateral_line]]
- [[running_deep_front_line]]
- [[running_cross_body_myofascial_hypothesis]]

## Evidence Gate

Require a repeated Level B pattern, a supported Level C interpretation, sufficient measurement confidence, and explicit hypothesis wording under [[running_evidence_gate]].

## Direct Assessment / Retest

Repeat the video under matched conditions and pair it with symptom history plus direct clinical assessment appropriate to the candidate structure.

## Camera Boundary

No camera view measures fascial force, tension, stiffness, contribution, restriction, or muscle activation. Camera view requirements belong to the upstream measurements and interpretations.

## Permitted Reporting Language

"This repeated movement pattern may support a Myofascial Hypothesis Layer hypothesis requiring clinical correlation."

## Forbidden Claims

- "Myofascial Hypothesis Layer is tight."
- "Myofascial Hypothesis Layer dysfunction was detected."
- Any treatment prescription derived from video alone.
