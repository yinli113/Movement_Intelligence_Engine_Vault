# Running - Functional Lines

## Definition

A running-domain hypothesis node connecting opposite limb and trunk coordination to existing Functional Line models.

## Why the Line Is Considered

Functional Lines provide cross-body anatomical relationships; they do not prove force transfer from camera motion.

## Required Biomechanical Interpretation

- [[running_altered_rotational_coordination]]
- [[running_cross_body_coordination]]
- [[running_arm_leg_coordination]]

## Hypothesis

A repeated opposite arm-leg timing difference may make front or back Functional Line relationships candidates for clinical correlation.

## Evidence Gate

Require a repeated Level B pattern, a supported Level C interpretation, sufficient measurement confidence, and explicit hypothesis wording under [[running_evidence_gate]].

## Direct Assessment / Retest

Repeat matched trials and assess trunk-to-contralateral-limb coordination and relevant joint capacity directly.

## Camera Boundary

No camera view measures fascial force, tension, stiffness, contribution, restriction, or muscle activation. Camera view requirements belong to the upstream measurements and interpretations.

## Permitted Reporting Language

"This repeated movement pattern may support a Functional Lines hypothesis requiring clinical correlation."

## Forbidden Claims

- "Functional Lines is tight."
- "Functional Lines dysfunction was detected."
- Any treatment prescription derived from video alone.
