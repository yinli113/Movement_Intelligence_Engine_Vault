# Running - Lateral Line

## Definition

A running-domain hypothesis node linking persistent frontal-plane or lateral-control patterns to the existing Lateral Line model.

## Why the Line Is Considered

The [[lateral_line]] is an anatomical framework for lateral continuity; camera data does not measure its loading.

## Required Biomechanical Interpretation

- [[running_different_load_distribution_strategy]]
- [[running_left_right_asymmetry]]

## Hypothesis

A persistent frontal-plane asymmetry with adequate view and context may make Lateral Line relationships a candidate hypothesis.

## Evidence Gate

Require a repeated Level B pattern, a supported Level C interpretation, sufficient measurement confidence, and explicit hypothesis wording under [[running_evidence_gate]].

## Direct Assessment / Retest

Repeat front/back trials and perform direct lateral trunk, pelvis, hip, and foot assessment relevant to the observed pattern.

## Camera Boundary

No camera view measures fascial force, tension, stiffness, contribution, restriction, or muscle activation. Camera view requirements belong to the upstream measurements and interpretations.

## Permitted Reporting Language

"This repeated movement pattern may support a Lateral Line hypothesis requiring clinical correlation."

## Forbidden Claims

- "Lateral Line is tight."
- "Lateral Line dysfunction was detected."
- Any treatment prescription derived from video alone.
