# Running literature claim audit — 19 October 2026

Scope: all 35 source notes in Running/08_Literature and the running app’s current narrative citations. This is an abstract/metadata and claim-applicability audit, not a full-text systematic review. 34 research sources were identified through public bibliographic records (Reinking additionally through PMC); one commercial commentary was read at its original page. Existing prior full-text work is retained but is not represented as newly repeated.

No reviewed source establishes validation of the current TillYes measurement algorithm or an athlete-specific causal conclusion. This means no such validation was established in this audit, not proof that no validation could ever exist.

## Corrections and open evidence
- Corrected Reinking authors, volume/pages and tested-variable scope; removed the unsupported automated-tracking advantage.
- Corrected the app’s economy-review/impact-shock citation mismatch and removed causal braking claims from image offsets.
- Corrected the Lang upper-body study’s sensor description, narrowed Larsen/Spataro claims, and preserved Pontzer–Arellano differences.
- Resolved author placeholders from research records; stored online versus issue dates separately. Legacy IDs remain unchanged. Winter is Sara, not Samantha. The mass-spring overview is indexed in the 2011 issue despite its legacy 2012 ID.
- Larsen publisher and abstract sample counts disagree; exact participant counts require reconciliation before reuse.
- Full-text extraction remains outstanding for sources checked only at abstract level. The van Oeveren knee-flexion passage remains publisher-indexed rather than a new complete full-text review.
- Myers remains an anatomical framework; Movaia/Gruenke are practice commentary, not experimental validation.
