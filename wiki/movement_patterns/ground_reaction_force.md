# Ground Reaction Force

## Definition

Ground Reaction Force is the external force the ground applies back into the body when the body applies force into the ground.

## Why It Matters

It connects the movement graph to external forces. In golf, the body does not create club speed from muscles alone; it organizes pressure, force transmission, and sequencing from the ground upward.
