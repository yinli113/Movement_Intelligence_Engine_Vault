# Energy Transfer

## Definition

Energy transfer is the movement of mechanical or elastic energy from one part of the system to another during a coordinated action.

## Why It Matters

In golf, the graph should reason about where energy is stored, redirected, released, or lost through compensation.

## Supporting Evidence From Source

The [[golf_decoded_six_phases_swing]] source identifies movement intervals around backswing, unweighting, and impact. The graph interprets these intervals as candidates for energy storage and release, but this specific energetic interpretation needs additional evidence.
