## Why It Matters

This source helps the graph reason about golf movement as a sequence of phase intervals, not isolated positions. It introduces boundary events such as [[end_pelvis_rotation]] and [[max_unweighting]] that are useful for force transmission, energy storage, and timing analysis.

## Supporting Evidence From Source

- The [source video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eDFQrVB2PXc&t=206s) presents the six-phase swing model used by this graph.
- The screenshot title reads "The 6 Phases of the Swing".
- The visible phase labels are:
  - Address -> Shaft Parallel
  - Shaft Parallel -> End Pelvis Rotation
  - End Pelvis Rotation -> Top of Backswing
  - Top Backswing -> Max Unweighting
  - Max Unweighting -> Impact
  - Impact -> Hands Chest Height
- The caption says, "Dynamic movement is a dynamic movement," supporting the graph principle that phases should be modeled as moving intervals rather than static snapshots.
