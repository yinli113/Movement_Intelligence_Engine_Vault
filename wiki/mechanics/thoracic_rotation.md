# Biomechanical Action: Thoracic Rotation

Thoracic rotation is rib cage rotation over the pelvis. In golf, it is the trunk mechanic that lets the player create separation without forcing the neck, lumbar spine, or trail shoulder to fake rotation.

## Golf Reasoning Role
During [[golf_swing_transition]], the pelvis begins to unwind while the thorax finishes loading. Good thoracic rotation allows elastic tension through the [[spiral_line]] and [[functional_line]] without excessive bracing.

## Failure Patterns
- Lumbar spine twists because the rib cage does not rotate.
- Trail shoulder lifts or externally rotates late to create the missing turn.
- Neck tension and jaw clenching appear near the top of backswing or transition.
- Pelvis sways because trunk separation is not available.

## Reasoning Prompt
If shoulder or neck symptoms appear during the swing, check whether thoracic rotation is distributing motion before treating the symptom as isolated.
