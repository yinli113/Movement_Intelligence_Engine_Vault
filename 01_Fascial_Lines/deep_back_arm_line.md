# Deep Back Arm Line

## Definition

The Deep Back Arm Line is retained as a provisional legacy graph mapping from scapular and rotator-cuff structures to the posterior arm.

## Why It Matters

It provides the vault's deep posterior bridge from rib cage and scapula through the shoulder to the arm without asserting measured cuff activity or tissue loading.

## Stable Anatomy (Level 1 & 2)

The listed membership is a provisional legacy graph mapping whose exact Anatomy Trains locator has not been verified. The current node conservatively records [[rhomboids]], [[triceps_brachii]], [[supraspinatus]], [[infraspinatus]], [[teres_minor]], and [[subscapularis]], spanning the [[shoulder_joint]] and [[elbow_joint]] with proximal relationships to the [[thoracic_spine]] and scapula; it is not settled Level 1 support. The provisional mapping does not establish stabilisation, activation, or load in golf.
