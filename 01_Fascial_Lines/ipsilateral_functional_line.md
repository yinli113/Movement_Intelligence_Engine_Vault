# Ipsilateral Functional Line

## Definition

The Ipsilateral Functional Line is a same-side Anatomy Trains pathway linking the upper limb, lateral trunk, pelvis, and thigh.

## Why It Matters

It gives the vault a stable anatomical route for interpreting same-side trunk/hip organisation without inferring a measured line state from visible side bend or sway.

## Stable Anatomy (Level 1 & 2)

The line tracks shaft of humerus -> [[latissimus_dorsi]] -> ipsilateral [[external_oblique]] -> anterior superior iliac spine -> [[sartorius]] -> pes anserinus. Structural continuity does not establish loading, activation, or a stabilising effect in an individual swing.

## Golf Application Interpretation (Level 3 & 4 context)

Kwon describes the external mechanics; the following line mapping is the vault's Anatomy Trains-based golf interpretation.

The explicit bridge is ipsilateral trunk/hip linkage -> [[ipsilateral_functional_line]] -> rib cage/[[shoulder_joint]]. Measured GRF and moments can describe external mechanics, but visible sway or side bend does not identify the responsible tissue.

## App Hypotheses (Level 5)

Same-side acromion-to-hip distance, trunk side-bend angle, and pelvis translation may be camera-derived descriptors when view and landmarks permit. These descriptors do not measure IFL activity, fascial tension, loading, force, or energy and cannot diagnose a stabilisation failure.
