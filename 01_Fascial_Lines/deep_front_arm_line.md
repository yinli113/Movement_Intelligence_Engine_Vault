# Deep Front Arm Line

## Definition

The Deep Front Arm Line is an Anatomy Trains pathway linking deep anterior chest and shoulder structures to the anterior arm.

## Why It Matters

It gives the vault a deep anterior route from rib cage and shoulder to the upper limb while preserving the boundary between anatomy and golf interpretation.

## Stable Anatomy (Level 1 & 2)

The current node records [[pectoralis_minor]], [[subclavius]], and [[biceps_brachii]] and crosses the [[shoulder_joint]] and [[elbow_joint]]. Distal anatomy should not be expanded without a precise source locator. Stable membership does not establish shoulder anchoring, activation, or tissue loading in a swing.

## Golf Application Interpretation (Level 3 & 4 context)

Kwon describes the external mechanics; the following line mapping is the vault's Anatomy Trains-based golf interpretation.

The bridge is rib cage/[[thoracic_spine]] -> anterior scapular/shoulder region -> [[deep_front_arm_line]] -> elbow/hand, with a contextual connection to the [[front_functional_line]]. Kwon's external mechanics do not establish line-specific loading or club-force transmission.

## App Hypotheses (Level 5)

Camera-derived shoulder, elbow, wrist, and hand positions and angles are kinematic descriptors. Ordinary video cannot measure deep-arm loading, muscle activation, force, joint moment, fascial tension, or energy. No descriptor may be used to diagnose shoulder instability or direct treatment.
