# Static Posture Switch Failure Modes

## Overview

In static posture bodyreading (*Anatomy Trains* & Julie Hammond framework), **Switches** represent key fascial junctions where tensile load transitions between opposing sagittal, frontal, or transverse lines. A static posture type (Lordotic, Kyphotic-Lordotic, Flat-Back, Sway-Back) or frontal alignment imbalance reflects a persistent **Switch Failure Mode** where one track maintains tonic dominance over its antagonist.

All static posture switch failure relationships are **Level 5 Engine Synthesis** hypotheses. They describe observable postural alignment descriptors, not measured tissue pathology or medical diagnoses.

---

## 1. Suboccipital Cervical Switch (SBL vs. DFL Neck Flexors)

* **Associated Meridians:** [[superficial_back_line]] (SBL) & [[deep_front_line]] (DFL)
* **Anatomical Junction:** Occipital ridge and $C1-C7$ cervical vertebrae.
* **Normal Function:** Suboccipitals (SBL Local) and Longus Colli / Capitis (DFL Local) maintain head balance over the cervical spine.
* **Failure Mode (Forward Head Lock):** When thoracic kyphosis or forward trunk posture occurs, the suboccipital track (SBL) hyper-extends the upper cervical spine to preserve horizontal gaze, while DFL deep cervical flexors lose tone $\rightarrow$ resulting in **Forward Head Posture / Chin Jut**.

---

## 2. ASIS Pelvic Sagittal Switch (SFL Rectus Femoris vs. SFL Abdominal Wall)

* **Associated Meridian:** [[superficial_front_line]] (SFL)
* **Anatomical Junction:** Anterior Superior Iliac Spine (ASIS) station.
* **Normal Function:** Rectus Abdominis (SFL) pulls ASIS upward while Rectus Femoris (SFL) pulls downward, holding pelvic tilt neutral.
* **Failure Mode (Anterior Pelvic Tilt Shift):** Rectus Abdominis fails to anchor ASIS, allowing Rectus Femoris and Iliopsoas (DFL) to pull the pelvis into persistent **Anterior Pelvic Tilt** (Lordotic / Kyphotic-Lordotic postures).

---

## 3. Lumbar Erector / Psoas Switch (SBL Lumbar vs. DFL Psoas)

* **Associated Meridians:** [[superficial_back_line]] (SBL) & [[deep_front_line]] (DFL)
* **Anatomical Junction:** Lumbar spine ($L1-L5$) and thoracolumbar fascia.
* **Normal Function:** SBL erector spinae and DFL psoas/quadratus lumborum maintain sagittal lumbar lordosis curvature.
* **Failure Mode (Sway-Back / Flat-Back Shift):** 
  * *Flat-Back:* SBL erectors and DFL psoas lose lordotic tone, flattening the lumbar curve.
  * *Sway-Back:* Pelvis shifts anteriorly relative to thorax, resting on passive Y-ligaments; SBL upper erecters brace while lower DFL core under-engages.

---

## 4. Calcaneal-Navicular Tripod Switch (DFL Arch vs. LL Peroneal Track)

* **Associated Meridians:** [[deep_front_line]] (DFL) & [[lateral_line]] (LL)
* **Anatomical Junction:** Navicular, Medial Malleolus, and Calcaneus stations.
* **Normal Function:** Tibialis Posterior (DFL) lifts the medial arch while Peroneus Longus (LL) stabilizes the lateral foot edge.
* **Failure Mode (Static Foot Pronation / Supination):** DFL Tibialis Posterior underactivity allows the navicular station to drop, causing static **Medial Arch Collapse (Pronation)**.

## Vault Report Boundary & Clinical Rules

When referencing static posture switch failure modes in app reports:
1. **Never declare tissue impairment:** Use *"Posture alignment is consistent with an ASIS Pelvic Sagittal Switch imbalance"*, never *"Measured pelvic dysfunction"*.
2. **Provide non-structural alternatives:** Always mention habit, fatigue, footwear, or setup factors per [[czaprowski_nonstructural_posture_2018]].
3. **Include retest prompt:** Cue active postural re-alignment to test if posture corrects voluntarily.
