# Squat Switch Concepts: Anatomy and Hypothesis Boundary

## Source-grounded anatomy

Myers' *Anatomy Trains*, local PDF p.82 (printed p.68), describes fascial tracks joining/diverging at “switches.” Local PDF p.95 (printed p.81) describes the gastrocnemius–hamstring continuity becoming less coupled with knee flexion. Local PDF p.198 describes anterior/posterior fascial tracks around the adductors.

These are anatomical-framework descriptions. “Derailment” in this context is an exception to the model's track rules, not a camera-detected pathological failure. The checked passages do not establish a 30° knee-failure threshold, a 90° adductor switch classifier or five validated squat failure modes.

## Withdrawn causal translation — 2026-10-28

The former Knee, Femoral Adductor, ASIS Anchor, Medial Arch and Calcaneal Station failure rules are withdrawn as squat detectors. They do not establish muscle inhibition/overactivity, an ankle block, hip clearance, lumbar curvature or fascial loading from a pose trajectory. Bony “stations” are framework navigation terms, not evidence of a direct muscle attachment at that station.
