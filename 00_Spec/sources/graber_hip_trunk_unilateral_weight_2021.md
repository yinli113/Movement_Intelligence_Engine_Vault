# Hip and Trunk Muscle Activity and Mechanics During Walking With and Without Unilateral Weight

Graber KA, Loverro KL, Baldwin M, Nelson-Wong E, Tanor J, Lewis CL (2021). *Journal of Applied Biomechanics 37(4):351–358*. [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34051700/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1123/jab.2020-0273).

## Direct evidence

Twenty-six healthy adults; treadmill walking with and without unilateral hand-held weight. Contralateral to the carried weight, hip abductor activity increased while pelvic drop decreased and trunk lean increased. Ipsilateral hip abductor activity did not change. Pelvis, trunk and muscle activity respond to task context.

## Transfer boundary

A frontal tilt or lean does not identify hip abductor weakness. Loaded treadmill results do not prescribe an exercise for an unweighted recording. The personal application remains an assessment hypothesis; no 2D normal/abnormal threshold is supplied.
