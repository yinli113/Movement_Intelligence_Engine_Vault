## Overview & Scientific Value

The **Foundational Model of Anatomy (FMA)** is an evolving domain ontology that represents the entire phenotypic structure of human anatomy. Developed and maintained by the Structural Informatics Group at the University of Washington, it is the international gold standard for bio-ontological representation.
