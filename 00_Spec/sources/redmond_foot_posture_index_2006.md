# Development and validation of a novel rating system for scoring standing foot posture: The Foot Posture Index (FPI-6)

## Source Role

This source is a **Level 1 foundational domain taxonomy** for standing foot posture assessment. It defines the validated 6-item Foot Posture Index (FPI-6), establishing objective clinical criteria to classify foot posture across the continuum from pronation to neutral to supination.

## The 6 Validated Criteria

1. **Talar Head Palpation (Rearfoot)**: Palpation of talar head on medial and lateral aspects.
2. **Supra and Infra-malleolar Curvature (Rearfoot)**: Comparison of curvature above and below the lateral malleolus.
3. **Calcaneal Frontal Plane Position (Rearfoot)**: Calcaneus inversion / eversion angle relative to the lower leg vertical axis.
4. **Bulging in Region of Talonavicular Joint (Midfoot)**: Contour convexity or concavity at the talonavicular joint.
5. **Height and Congruence of Medial Longitudinal Arch (Midfoot)**: Medial arch profile and concentric curvature.
6. **Abduction / Adduction of Forefoot on Rearfoot (Forefoot)**: Medial vs lateral toe visibility ("too many toes" sign) from a posterior vantage point.

## Scoring System

Each criterion is scored on a 5-point integer scale:
- `-2`: Marked signs of supination
- `-1`: Moderate signs of supination
- `0`: Neutral / normal posture
- `+1`: Moderate signs of pronation
- `+2`: Marked signs of pronation

Aggregate FPI-6 Score = sum of all 6 items (Range: -12 to +12).

### Categorical Normative Bands
- **-12 to -5**: Highly Supinated
- **-4 to -1**: Supinated
- **0 to +5**: Normal / Neutral
- **+6 to +9**: Pronated
- **+10 to +12**: Highly Pronated
