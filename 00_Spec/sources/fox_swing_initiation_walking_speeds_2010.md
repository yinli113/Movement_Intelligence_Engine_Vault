# Contributions of muscles and passive dynamics to swing initiation over a range of walking speeds

Fox MD, Delp SL (2010). *Journal of Biomechanics 43(8):1450–1455*. [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20236644/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2010.02.009).

## Direct evidence

Eight unimpaired children and adolescents, ages 7–18; four walking speeds. Hip flexors contributed substantially to preswing knee acceleration, with contributions changing across walking speeds. Passive dynamics and multiple muscles also contributed to swing initiation.

## Transfer boundary

Paediatric model findings supply mechanistic context, not adult normative angles or individual muscle attribution. The personal application remains an assessment hypothesis; no 2D normal/abnormal threshold is supplied.
