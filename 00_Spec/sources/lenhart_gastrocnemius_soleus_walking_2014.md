# Empirical evaluation of gastrocnemius and soleus function during walking

Lenhart RL, Francis CA, Lenz AL, Thelen DG (2014). *Journal of Biomechanics 47(12):2969–2974*. [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25107666/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2014.07.007).

## Direct evidence

Twenty healthy young adults; brief electrical stimulation during stance. Gastrocnemius stimulation induced hip and knee flexion; midstance stimulation also induced ankle dorsiflexion. Soleus stimulation in midstance induced knee extension and plantarflexion. Responses depended on stimulation timing; gastrocnemius model predictions were sensitive to moment-arm assumptions.

## Transfer boundary

Anatomical action labels alone cannot predict whole-limb walking effects. This study did not test clinical calf tightness. The personal application remains an assessment hypothesis; no 2D normal/abnormal threshold is supplied.
