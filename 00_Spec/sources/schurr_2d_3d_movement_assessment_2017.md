# Schurr et al. (2017): 2D and 3D movement assessment

Int J Sports Phys Ther. 12(2):163–172. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5380858/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28515970/). Reviewed online 2026-09-30; no local full-text archive claimed.

## Direct evidence

Descriptive laboratory study: 26 healthy, recreationally active adults performed three single-leg squats per limb. Simultaneous 2D cameras and 3D motion capture compared joint displacement. Sagittal correlations were trunk 0.53, hip 0.93, knee 0.86 and ankle 0.51. Markers helped identify sagittal landmarks. Measurements compared starting position with peak knee flexion, not event sequences. Agreement limits were wide, particularly at the trunk; rotation was not captured by 2D.
