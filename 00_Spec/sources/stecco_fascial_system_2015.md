# Stecco's Functional Atlas of the Human Fascial System (2015)

## Overview & Scientific Value

Authored by Dr. Carla Stecco (Orthopaedic Surgeon and Professor of Human Anatomy at the University of Padova), this landmark atlas provides systematic macroscopic and histological photographic evidence of the human fascial system from non-embalmed cadaveric dissections.

### Key Findings & Relevance to the Vault:
1. **Myofascial Expansions**: Demonstrates that skeletal muscles do not insert solely onto bones via isolated tendons, but send prominent fascial expansions (e.g., gluteus maximus into the fascia lata / IT band; pectoralis major into the brachial fascia; lacertus fibrosus from biceps brachii into deep antebrachial fascia).
2. **Deep Fascial Organization**: Classifies deep muscular fascia as a continuous multi-layered connective tissue specialized for transmitting tension across adjacent segments.
3. **Mechanical Coupling**: Corroborates Thomas Myers' Anatomy Trains hypothesis by verifying continuous tissue planes across muscular boundaries.
