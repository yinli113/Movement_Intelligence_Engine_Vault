# Muscles that influence knee flexion velocity in double support: implications for stiff-knee gait

Goldberg SR, Anderson FC, Pandy MG, Delp SL (2004). *Journal of Biomechanics 37(8):1189–1196*. [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15212924/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2003.12.005).

## Direct evidence

Normal walking simulation; muscle-force perturbations during double support. In this model, iliopsoas and gastrocnemius increased knee flexion velocity before swing; vasti, rectus femoris and soleus decreased it. The effects depend on limb configuration and model assumptions.

## Transfer boundary

These dynamic effects do not establish tightness, weakness or activation from a knee curve. The personal application remains an assessment hypothesis; no 2D normal/abnormal threshold is supplied.
