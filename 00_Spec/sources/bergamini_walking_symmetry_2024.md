# Walking symmetry is speed and index dependent

Bergamini E, Cereatti A, Pavei G (2024). [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-69461-w). Reviewed online 13 November 2026; no local PDF archive claimed.

## Direct evidence and transfer boundary

Ten healthy adults, seven treadmill speeds and a simulated-asymmetry condition. Symmetry depended on the selected index, direction and conditions; symmetry angle showed no significant speed effect in this sample. Supports descriptive, metric-specific comparisons. Does not establish a camera asymmetry cutoff, perfect symmetry as a norm or diagnosis.
