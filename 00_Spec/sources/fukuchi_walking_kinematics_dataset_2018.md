# Fukuchi et al. (2018): Walking Kinematics and Kinetics Dataset

*PeerJ*. 2018;6:e4640. [Full text (PMC5922232)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5922232/) · [PubMed (29707431)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29707431/). Reviewed online 2026-11-13; no local PDF archive claimed.

## Direct evidence

Open-access 3D motion-capture and force-plate study providing lower-extremity and pelvic joint angular kinematics (including sagittal hip flexion/extension, knee flexion/extension, and ankle dorsiflexion/plantarflexion) and joint kinetics across overground and treadmill walking at multiple speeds in healthy adults.

## Transfer boundary

Supports normative **3D instrumented** sagittal joint-angle progressions (ankle dorsiflexion/plantarflexion, knee flexion/extension, hip flexion/extension) across the gait cycle as general biomechanical context. Does **not** validate markerless 2D webcam/MediaPipe angles as equivalent to 3D marker-based motion capture, nor does it authorize causal or kinetic claims from 2D video. See [[gait_observability_boundary]].
