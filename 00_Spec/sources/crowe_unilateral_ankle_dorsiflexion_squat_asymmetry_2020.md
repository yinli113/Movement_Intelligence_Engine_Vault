# Unilateral ankle restriction and squat force asymmetry

Twenty healthy physically active adults performed barefoot bilateral bodyweight squats with and without a ten-degree right forefoot wedge imposing an artificial ankle dorsiflexion restriction. Force platforms showed greater vertical force from the unrestricted limb and increased interlimb force asymmetry across the studied phases.

This directly supports a force-distribution effect under the experimental restriction. It does not establish that a visible lateral hip shift is caused by ankle restriction, identify a restricted anatomical side from camera direction, or validate any knee-to-wall cutoff. A suggestion to compare ankle range in someone investigating a hip shift is an explicitly qualified assessment hypothesis, not a finding of this study.
