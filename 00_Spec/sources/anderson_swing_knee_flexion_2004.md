# Contributions of muscle forces and toe-off kinematics to peak knee flexion during the swing phase of normal gait: an induced position analysis

Anderson FC, Goldberg SR, Pandy MG, Delp SL (2004). *Journal of Biomechanics 37(5):731–737*. [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15047002/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2003.09.018).

## Direct evidence

Normal walking represented by a three-dimensional muscle-actuated simulation. The simulation attributed much of swing knee flexion to knee flexion velocity at toe-off. Muscle contributions interacted rather than mapping one joint peak to one muscle.

## Transfer boundary

A model contribution is not measured muscle activity or a validated camera cutoff. The personal application remains an assessment hypothesis; no 2D normal/abnormal threshold is supplied.
