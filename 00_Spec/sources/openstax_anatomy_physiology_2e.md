# OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e

## Source Role

OpenStax *Anatomy and Physiology 2e* serves as the vault's open-access anatomical reference authority for standard physical structure definitions:
1. **Musculoskeletal Attachments**: Authoritative source for proximal attachments (origins) and distal attachments (insertions) across all appendicular and axial muscles.
2. **Neurological Supply**: Canonical peripheral nerve and spinal root innervation mappings.
3. **Open Access Standards**: CC BY 4.0 licensed reference enabling reproducible data models across the TillYes engine and client applications.

## Chapter & Section Mappings for Muscular System (Unit 3, Chapter 11)

- **§11.1**: Interactions of Skeletal Muscles, Their Fascicle Arrangement, and Their Lever Systems.
- **§11.2**: Naming Skeletal Muscles.
- **§11.3**: Axial Muscles of the Head, Neck, and Back (Erector Spinae, Multifidus, Splenius, Suboccipitals).
- **§11.4**: Axial Muscles of the Abdominal Wall and Thorax (Obliques, Transversus, Rectus Abdominis, Diaphragm, Intercostals).
- **§11.5**: Muscles of the Pectoral Girdle and Upper Limbs (Trapezius, Rhomboids, Serratus Anterior, Rotator Cuff, Arm & Forearm Compartments).
- **§11.6**: Appendicular Muscles of the Pelvic Girdle and Lower Limbs (Gluteals, Iliopsoas, Adductor Group, Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Deep Rotators, Crural Compartments).
