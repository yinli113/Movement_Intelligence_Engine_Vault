# Kadaba et al. (1990): Lower Extremity Kinematics During Level Walking

*J Orthop Res*. 1990;8(3):383–392. [PubMed (2324857)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2324857/). Reviewed online 2026-11-13; no local PDF archive claimed.

## Direct evidence

Instrumented 3D video motion-analysis study of 40 healthy young adults tested across three sessions during level walking. Established continuous normative joint-angle waveforms across 0–100% of the gait cycle for the pelvis, hip, knee, and ankle in sagittal, frontal, and transverse planes (precursor to the Conventional Gait Model). Demonstrated that sagittal-plane flexion/extension curves are highly repeatable, whereas frontal- and transverse-plane rotations are substantially more sensitive to axis-alignment and marker-placement errors.

## Transfer boundary

Supports general normative sagittal-plane joint-angle ranges (ankle dorsiflexion/plantarflexion, knee flexion/extension, hip flexion/extension) across the gait cycle and reinforces why out-of-plane rotations cannot be reliably quantified from single-camera 2D views. Does **not** validate markerless 2D video tracking against 3D marker-based kinematics. See [[gait_observability_boundary]].
