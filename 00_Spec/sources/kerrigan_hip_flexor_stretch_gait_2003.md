# Effect of a hip flexor-stretching program on gait in the elderly

Kerrigan DC, Xenopoulos-Oddsson A, Sullivan MJ, Lelas JJ, Riley PO (2003). *Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation 84(1):1–6*. [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12589613/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1053/apmr.2003.50056).

## Direct evidence

Ninety-six healthy older adults; randomized ten-week stretching comparison. Static hip extension improved within the intervention group. Comfortable-speed and fast-speed dynamic hip-extension changes were trends (P=.103 and .093), not statistically significant improvements. Available passive movement and walking excursion are distinct outcomes.

## Transfer boundary

Small walking excursion does not establish hip-flexor shortening. The trial does not support promising that stretching changes this app pattern. The personal application remains an assessment hypothesis; no 2D normal/abnormal threshold is supplied.
