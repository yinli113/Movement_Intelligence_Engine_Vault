# Stance Phase (Gait)

The ~62% of the gait cycle from foot strike to toe-off: initial double-limb support (0-12%, loading/weight transfer), single-limb stance (12-50%, support + COM moving forward), second double-limb support/preswing (50-62%, unloading).
