# Squat Myofascial Mapping & Switch Failure Taxonomy

## Evidence Status

Anatomy Trains defines structural continuities but does not provide a specific camera-based squat scoring model or single-line diagnosis. A bodyweight squat loads multiple myofascial meridians simultaneously across closed kinetic chains. Every metric-to-line relationship in this node is an **Engine Synthesis** (Level 5), presenting testable clinical hypotheses rather than detected tissue impairments.

See [[squat_switch_failure_modes]] for the foundational mechanics of dynamic fascial derailments during compound movement.

---

## The 21-Misalignment Squat Myofascial Taxonomy

Below is the comprehensive matrix linking camera-observable kinematic findings to myofascial line pairs, bony stations, dynamic switch failures, express vs. local muscle dynamics, and in-person retest protocols.

### 1. Foot & Ankle Complex

| Observable Pattern | Anatomical Continuity & Lines | Bony Station Anchor | Switch Failure Mode | Express vs. Local Dynamics | Retest Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Excessive Foot Pronation / Arch Collapse** | [[deep_front_line]] (Tibialis Posterior) & [[spiral_line]] (Foot Loop) | Navicular / Medial Malleolus | [[squat_switch_failure_modes#4-the-medial-arch-stirrup-switch-dfl-spl-foot-loop\|Medial Arch Stirrup Switch]] | Tibialis Posterior (Local DFL) arch support fails; Peroneus Longus (Express SPL) pulls 1st metatarsal into eversion. | Place 5mm wedge under 1st metatarsal or cue active short foot tripod. |
| **Excessive Foot Supination / Rigid High Arch** | [[lateral_line]] (Peroneals) & [[superficial_back_line]] (Lateral Plantar) | Cuboid / 5th Metatarsal Base | Lateral Line Eversion Switch Lock | Peroneus Longus (Express LL) locks foot in inversion, blocking Tibialis Posterior (Local DFL) ground adaptation. | Cue tripod foot grounding (pressing big toe joint into floor). |
| **Toe Elevation / Toe Clawing** | [[superficial_front_line]] (Extensor Digitorum) vs [[deep_front_line]] (Flexor Digitorum) | Distal Phalanges | Ankle Dorsiflexion Emergency Switch | Extensor/Flexor Digitorum (Expresses) substitute for blocked Tibialis Anterior / Soleus (Locals). | Place coin under 1st toe pad; retest without curling or lifting toe. |
| **Early Heel Lift** | [[superficial_back_line]] (Soleus/Achilles) & [[deep_front_line]] (Tibialis Posterior) | Calcaneus (Heel Anchor) | [[squat_switch_failure_modes#5-the-calcaneal-station-elevation-switch-sbl-dfl-ankle-block\|Calcaneal Station Elevation Switch]] | Soleus (Local SBL) or Tibialis Posterior (Local DFL) tightness forces calcaneal station elevation to bypass ankle block. | Elevate heels on 20mm wedge. If depth normalizes, confirms distal calf block. |
| **Asymmetrical Foot Flare** | [[spiral_line]] (Diagonal Sling) & [[deep_front_line]] (Deep Rotators) | Greater Trochanter / Sacrum | Asymmetric Transverse Sling Switch | Deep Rotators (Locals) locked short on one side to bypass unilateral hip/ankle dorsiflexion restriction. | Align both feet to 0° forward facing and measure hip depth asymmetry. |

### 2. Knee & Lower Leg Complex

| Observable Pattern | Anatomical Continuity & Lines | Bony Station Anchor | Switch Failure Mode | Express vs. Local Dynamics | Retest Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dynamic Knee Valgus** | [[deep_front_line]] (Adductors) & [[lateral_line]] (Glute Medius) | Linea Aspera / Femoral Condyle | [[squat_switch_failure_modes#2-the-femoral-adductor-switch-dfl-anterior-vs-posterior-track\|Femoral Adductor Switch]] | Gluteus Medius (Local LL) inhibited; Tensor Fasciae Latae (Express LL) and Adductors (DFL) pull femur medial. | Place mini-band above knees; retest if active abduction clears inward path. |
| **Dynamic Knee Varus** | [[lateral_line]] (TFL/ITB) & [[superficial_back_line]] (Biceps Femoris) | Gerdy's Tubercle / Fibular Head | Lateral Harness Switch Lock | Biceps Femoris long head (Express SBL) & TFL (Express LL) pull tibia into lateral rotation over DFL. | Squeeze foam roller between knees during descent to engage adductors. |
| **Quad-Dominant Shear / Excessive Knee Advance** | [[superficial_front_line]] (Rectus Femoris) vs [[superficial_back_line]] (Gluteus Max) | Tibial Tuberosity / AIIS | Quadriceps Deceleration Switch Lock | Rectus Femoris (Express SFL) dominates deceleration; Vastus Medialis/Lateralis & Glutes under-utilized. | Perform box squat / sit-back cueing to engage posterior chain. |
| **Restrained Knee Travel / Excessive Hip Hinge** | [[superficial_back_line]] (Distal Calf) & [[superficial_front_line]] (Tibialis Ant) | Calcaneus / Talus | Ankle Dorsiflexion Lockout Switch | Soleus (Local SBL) tightness blocks forward tibial travel, shifting 100% load to posterior hip hinge. | Elevate heels or measure passive ankle dorsiflexion off-load. |
| **Single-Knee Valgus / Knee Asymmetry** | [[spiral_line]] (Cross-Sling) & [[lateral_line]] (Unilateral Hip) | Opposite ASIS / Rib Cage | Rotational Sling Torque Failure | Cross-body Obliques (Express SPL) overload stance leg Gluteus Medius (Local LL). | Retest thoracic rotation and single-leg balance stability. |

### 3. Pelvis & Hip Complex

| Observable Pattern | Anatomical Continuity & Lines | Bony Station Anchor | Switch Failure Mode | Express vs. Local Dynamics | Retest Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Butt Wink (Posterior Pelvic Tilt at Depth)** | [[deep_front_line]] (Adductor Magnus) + [[superficial_back_line]] (Hamstrings) | Ischial Tuberosity / Ramus | [[squat_switch_failure_modes#2-the-femoral-adductor-switch-dfl-anterior-vs-posterior-track\|Adductor Magnus Length Switch]] | Adductor Magnus (DFL hybrid) reaches terminal length at deep flexion, tugging ischial tuberosity forward/down. | Widen stance 15% and externally rotate feet 10°. |
| **Anterior Pelvic Tilt ("Duck Butt")** | [[superficial_front_line]] (Rectus Femoris) & [[deep_front_line]] (Psoas) | ASIS / AIIS / Lesser Trochanter | [[squat_switch_failure_modes#3-the-asis-anchor-switch-sfl-pelvic-station\|ASIS Station Anchor Switch]] | Psoas/Iliacus (Express DFL) pulls ASIS down because Transversus/Rectus Abdominis (SFL) fails to anchor. | Cue "rib cage down" and active core bracing prior to descent. |
| **Pelvic Shift / Lateral Sway** | [[lateral_line]] (Unilateral Glute Med/QL) & [[deep_front_line]] (Adductors) | Greater Trochanter / PSIS | Unilateral Lateral Line Lock | Quadratus Lumborum (Express LL) hikes hip to compensate for weak stance Gluteus Medius (Local LL). | Perform supported split squat to isolate single-leg stability. |
| **Pelvic Rotation / Yaw Asymmetry** | [[spiral_line]] (Internal/External Oblique Sling) | ASIS / Sacroiliac Joint | Asymmetric Spiral Sling Switch | Obliques (Express SPL) twist pelvis over femoral heads due to asymmetrical hip internal rotation. | Measure seated passive hip internal/external rotation differential. |
| **Unlevel Hip Line / Asymmetrical Depth** | [[lateral_line]] (Quadratus Lumborum) & [[deep_front_line]] (Psoas) | Iliac Crest / L1-L5 | Unilateral QL Side-Lock Switch | Quadratus Lumborum (Express LL) holds one iliac crest elevated against opposite Pelvic Floor (Local DFL). | Retest side-plank endurance and QL passive length. |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## App Clinical Report Guidelines

When surfacing these findings in the Movement App:
1. **Always cite recorded metric trigger first:** e.g. *"Camera recorded 14° dynamic knee valgus at 80° flexion."*
2. **Present fascial line as candidate hypothesis:** *"May be consistent with a DFL-LL vector imbalance involving the Femoral Adductor Switch."*
3. **Include non-fascial joint explanations:** Mention joint capsule, motor strategy, stance width, or balance considerations.
4. **Prompt in-person retest:** Provide the retest protocol to confirm or rule out the hypothesis.
