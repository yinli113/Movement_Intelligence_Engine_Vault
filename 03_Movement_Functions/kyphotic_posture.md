# Kyphotic Posture

A non-structural sagittal posture type: increased thoracic kyphosis + head protraction + flattened/reversed lower cervical lordosis + increased upper cervical lordosis + shoulder/scapular protraction. Lengthened: thoracic erector spinae, rhomboids, serratus anterior, middle/lower trapezius. Shortened: suboccipitals, SCM, scalenes, pectorals, latissimus dorsi (insertion area). A **pattern hypothesis**, not a diagnosis.
