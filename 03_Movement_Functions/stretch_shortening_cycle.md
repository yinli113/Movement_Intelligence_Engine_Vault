# Stretch-Shortening Cycle

## Definition

The **stretch-shortening cycle (SSC)** describes a movement organised in three periods:

1. **Eccentric / lengthening preparation** — the relevant segments are carried into a lengthened, loaded configuration;
2. **Transition / coupling period** — the movement reverses direction; a short, well-timed coupling is associated with effective reuse of elastic behaviour, while a long pause dissipates it;
3. **Concentric / shortening release** — the segments shorten and the stored behaviour is expressed as motion.

## Why It Matters

The engine's question is whether a movement **creates, stores, and releases elastic behaviour smoothly** — not whether any single angle is large. The SSC gives that question a three-part temporal structure and connects golf (pelvis-thorax dissociation, reversal timing) to gait, squat, and other domains.

## Golf Observable Proxies (Level 5, indirect)

Possible camera-observable correlates of SSC organisation in a golf swing include:

- pelvis-thorax dissociation and its transition increase (see [[x_factor]]);
- timing of pelvis reversal and thorax reversal;
- functional-line length proxies (diagonal shoulder-hip distance descriptors);
- segment angular-velocity descriptors and their rates of change;
- rate of change of line-length proxies;
- COM-proxy displacement;
- lead- and trail-leg behaviour;
- arm and wrist sequencing;
- radius (hand-path) shortening and re-lengthening.

These are **indirect biomechanical proxies**. Two-dimensional video does not measure fascial tension, muscle force, stored elastic energy, or ground-reaction force; see [[golf_kinetics_observability_boundary]].

## Interpretation Layers

### Measured Mechanics — Levels 2–3

Eccentric-coupling-concentric structure is an established general model. Measured elastic-energy storage and return require instrumented work-power analysis, not video.

### Fascial Interpretation — Levels 1–2 plus explicit inference

Smooth SSC behaviour *may be consistent with* effective load transfer along [[functional_lines]] and related lines. That association is an interpretation, not a measurement of fascia.

### App Hypothesis — Level 5

The app may score SSC-like organisation from the proxies above (e.g., coupling duration, reversal smoothness). Such scores are Level 5 hypotheses and must be labelled as coordination descriptors, not elastic-energy measurements.

## Evidence Level

The general SSC model is Level 2; instrumented elastic-energy measurements are Level 3; video SSC proxies are Level 5.

## App Use

Report SSC organisation as timing and smoothness descriptors (preparation duration, coupling duration, release rate, reversal order). Do not report stored elastic energy, fascial recoil force, or muscle power from video.
