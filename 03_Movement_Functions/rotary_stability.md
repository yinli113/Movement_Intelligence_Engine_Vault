# Rotary Stability (FMS Test 7)

## Definition

**Rotary stability** is a quadruped contralateral (opposite-arm/opposite-leg) reach screening multi-plane trunk stability and shoulder/hip core control, scored 0-3 per side with a posterior-press clearing exam (Gray Cook, *Movement*, Ch. 6, Ch. 9). FMS test 7.

## Why it matters

Rotary/multi-plane trunk control is central to golf rotation, gait counter-rotation, and any sport needing anti-rotation stability. It is the most complex FMS coordination pattern.

## Source-derived model

- Quadruped; extend opposite arm and leg simultaneously; repeat with a diagonal knee-to-elbow touch for higher scores.
- Scored 0-3 per side; clearing exam checks for painful spine flexion.
