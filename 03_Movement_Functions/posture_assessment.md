# Posture Assessment

Assessment of standing posture. The vault's posture-assessment anchor is currently the foundational Czaprowski taxonomy (Level 1) plus the Anatomy Trains/Julie Hammond BodyReading philosophy (Level 1). Instrumented posture biomechanics (Level 3, e.g., Kendall photogrammetry) and applied clinical frameworks (Level 4, e.g., Sahrmann) are **future domain-expansion sources**, not required blockers for the MVP.
