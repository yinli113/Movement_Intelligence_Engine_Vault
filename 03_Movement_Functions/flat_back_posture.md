# Flat-Back Posture

A non-structural sagittal posture type: flattened lumbar lordosis + flattened lower thoracic kyphosis (± increased upper thoracic kyphosis / cervico-thoracic kyphotisation); pelvis neutral or decreased anterior tilt. Lengthened: lumbar erector spinae, iliacus, psoas, two-joint hip flexors. Shortened: gluteus maximus, hamstrings (hyperactive). A **pattern hypothesis**, not a diagnosis.
