# Hurdle Step (FMS Test 2)

## Definition

The **hurdle step** is a single-leg step over a low hurdle, screening [[hip_joint]] mobility and single-leg stance stability of the stance limb, scored 0-3 per side (Gray Cook, *Movement*, Ch. 6, Ch. 9). FMS test 2.

## Why it matters

Single-leg stance stability is shared across gait, golf lead-leg, and running. The hurdle step is a cross-domain bridge between the FMS screen and gait/single-leg mechanics.

## Source-derived model

- Step over a hurdle at set height; pelvis should stay level; trunk minimal sway.
- Scored 0-3 per side; asymmetry reduces the composite score.

## Joint involvement

[[hip_joint]] flexion of the stepping limb; [[ankle_joint]], [[knee_joint]], [[hip_joint]] stability of the stance limb.

## Muscle involvement

No muscle claims from this node.

## Movement or phase relationships

A fundamental [[movement_pattern]] (A); single-leg stance relates to gait single-limb support and golf lead-leg loading (C).

## Possible myofascial relationships

None directly. Fascial-line mapping is an engine synthesis (C).

## What a 2D app can observe

- Pelvic levelness, trunk sway, stepping-limb clearance height, knee tracking — from a 2D front/side view.

## What the app must not infer

- Which side's mobility or stability is deficient.
- Muscle activation, fascial tension, or diagnosis.
