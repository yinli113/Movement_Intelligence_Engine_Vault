# App Philosophy

The engine's guiding question is **not** "What posture do you have?" but **"How does your body organise movement?"** Three Level 1 sources jointly define this: [[czaprowski_nonstructural_posture_2018]] (cautious non-structural interpretation; hypo/hyperactivity over shortening/lengthening), [[julie_hammond_breakout]] (posture as a question; adaptability over idealised alignment), and [[gray_cook_movement_2010]] (movement patterns before body parts; screening vs diagnosis).
