# Global Stabilizers

Superficial, multi-articular antigravity muscles that maintain erected posture and stabilise joint position during movement. Members include middle/lower trapezius, lumbar erector spinae, iliacus, gluteus maximus, gluteus medius, adductor magnus, and adductor brevis.
