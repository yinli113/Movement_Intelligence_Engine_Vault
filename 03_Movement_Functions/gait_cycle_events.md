# Gait Cycle Events

The eight sequential events defining the cycle: foot strike (0%), opposite toe-off (12%), reversal of fore-aft shear, opposite foot strike (50%), toe-off (62%), foot clearance (75%), tibia vertical (85%), successive foot strike (100%). The older terms "heel strike" and "foot flat" are avoided because they may be absent in pathological gait.
