# Inline Lunge (FMS Test 3)

## Definition

The **inline lunge** places the feet along a narrow line and lunges, screening [[hip_joint]] mobility, [[knee_joint]] stability, and trunk control in an asymmetrical stance, scored 0-3 per side (Gray Cook, *Movement*, Ch. 6, Ch. 9). FMS test 3.

## Why it matters

The asymmetrical stance and narrow base stress single-leg control and hip/knee coordination — relevant to gait, golf weight shift, and change of direction.

## Source-derived model

- Feet aligned along a line; descend until the back knee touches a board behind the front heel.
- Scored 0-3 per side; asymmetry reduces the composite score.

## Joint involvement

[[hip_joint]] flexion/extension, [[knee_joint]] flexion, [[ankle_joint]] dorsiflexion, trunk control.
