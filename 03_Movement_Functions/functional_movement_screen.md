# Functional Movement Screen (FMS)

## Definition

The Functional Movement Screen is a **seven-test battery** that ranks the quality of fundamental movement patterns on a 0-3 scale, with three clearing exams. It is a **screen** — it sorts people by movement quality and flags pain — not a clinical assessment or a diagnosis (Gray Cook, *Movement*, Ch. 5-6).

## Why it matters

FMS supplies the engine's cross-domain screening template: the same seven patterns (deep squat, hurdle step, inline lunge, shoulder mobility, active straight-leg raise, trunk stability push-up, rotary stability) apply regardless of domain (golf, gait, posture, squat). It operationalises the philosophy that **movement quality precedes performance** and that **screening precedes assessment**.

## Source-derived model

- Seven tests, each scored 0-3, with asymmetry penalised.
- Three clearing exams (shoulder mobility, press-up, rotary stability) catch painful range missed by the scored test.
- Output is a pattern score and a pain flag, not a kinetic variable or a fascial membership.

## Joint involvement

Whole-body, multi-joint: [[deep_squat]] loads [[ankle_joint]], [[knee_joint]], [[hip_joint]], [[lumbar_spine]], [[thoracic_spine]], [[shoulder_joint]]; the other tests load single-leg, shoulder, and trunk chains respectively.

## Muscle involvement

FMS does not grade muscles. It grades patterns. Muscle-level interpretation is out of scope for this node and belongs to clinical assessment, not the screen.

## Movement or phase relationships

Each test is a fundamental [[movement_pattern]] (A). The screen sits under the sport-skill layer of the [[performance_pyramid]] (A) and is informed by the [[joint_by_joint_concept]] (A).

## Possible myofascial relationships

None directly from this source. Cross-linking fascial-line membership to an FMS test is an engine synthesis (C) and must not be reported as a measured relationship.

## What a 2D app can observe

- Joint angles and alignment proxies per test (e.g., knee valgus, torso lean, arm symmetry) from a 2D side/front view.
- Whether the pattern is completed, and an approximate 0-3 quality score by rule.

## What the app must not infer

- Underlying joint mobility or stability deficits (requires hands-on assessment).
- Muscle length, activation, or fascial tension.
- Pathology, pain source, or diagnosis.
- A kinetic or causal explanation of a low score.
