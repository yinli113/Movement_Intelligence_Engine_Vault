# Hypoactivity (Muscle)

Reflectory inhibition of a muscle in response to stress (joint injury, ballistic repetitive exercise, or lack of gravitational load/use), manifesting clinically as weakness. Local stabilisers are particularly prone to hypoactivity. **Distinct from lengthening** — a lengthened muscle is not necessarily hypoactive/weak, and a hypoactive muscle is not necessarily lengthened.
