# Gait Assessment

Assessment of an individual's gait. The vault's gait-assessment anchor is currently the foundational Chambers & Sutherland taxonomy (Level 1). Instrumented gait biomechanics (Level 3) and applied gait-coaching/practice frameworks (Level 4) are **future domain-expansion sources** (e.g., Perry & Burnfield, Whittle), not required blockers for the MVP.
