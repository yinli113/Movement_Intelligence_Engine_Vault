# Gait Cycle

One foot strike to the next foot strike of the same limb, normalised 0-100%. Stance ~62%, swing ~38%. Divided by eight events into three stance periods (initial double-limb support, single-limb stance, second double-limb support/preswing) and three swing phases (initial, mid, terminal).
