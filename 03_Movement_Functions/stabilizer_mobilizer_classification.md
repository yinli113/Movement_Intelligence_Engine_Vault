# Stabilizer/Mobilizer Functional Muscle Classification

The Bergmark/Richardson functional classification: **local stabilisers** (deep, mono-articular, joint-segment control, prone to reflectory inhibition/hypoactivity), **global stabilisers** (antigravity, multi-articular), and **mobilisers** (movement-producing, prone to reflectory excitation/hyperactivity and decreased flexibility). The core of the engine's "how does the body organise movement" philosophy: stabiliser hypoactivity triggers mobiliser hyperactivity compensation.
