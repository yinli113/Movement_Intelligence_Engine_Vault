# Sway-Back Posture

A non-structural sagittal posture type: anterior pelvic shift + thoracic kyphosis extending into upper lumbar spine + apparently shorter lumbar lordosis + normal/slightly decreased anterior pelvic tilt. Lengthened: upper thoracic/upper lumbar erector spinae, scapular stabilisers, lower abdominals, hip flexors. Shortened: suboccipitals, SCM, scalenes, pectorals, lower lumbar erector spinae, upper abdominals, gluteus maximus, hamstrings (mostly hyperactive). A **pattern hypothesis**, not a diagnosis.
