# Muscle Length/Strength Assessment

Clinical assessment of muscle length and strength (per Kendall et al.). Czaprowsi explicitly warns that the Kendall-style "lengthened = weak / short = strong" rule is insufficient — the hamstrings example shows a lengthened-but-hyperactive muscle, so length/strength assessment must be combined with the functional-organisation reading.
