# Mobilizers

Multi-articular movement-producing muscles not related to antigravity postural action. Members include thoracic erector spinae, rectus abdominis, external oblique, anterior internal oblique, lateral quadratus lumborum, psoas, hamstrings, TFL, rectus femoris, and adductor longus. Prone to reflectory excitation (hyperactivity) and decreased flexibility — **not** the same as true muscle-fibre shortening/contracture.
