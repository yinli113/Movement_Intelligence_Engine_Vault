# Force Transmission

## Definition

Force transmission is the transfer of force through connected body regions and tissues, including joints, muscles, connective structures, fascial lines, and the ground.

## Why It Matters

This concept is the bridge between the anatomy graph and movement performance. It prevents the system from reasoning only through isolated muscles.
