# Lordotic Posture

A non-structural sagittal posture type: increased lumbar lordosis + increased anterior pelvic tilt; knees may hyperextend and ankles plantarflex. Lengthened: abdominals, gluteus maximus, posterior gluteus medius, hamstrings. Shortened: quadratus lumborum, hip flexors (iliacus/psoas/rectus femoris/TFL). Gluteus maximus hypoactive; hamstrings hyperactive (compensating). A **pattern hypothesis**, not a diagnosis.
