# Lordotic Posture

## Definition

**Lordotic posture** is a non-structural sagittal posture type defined by **increased lumbar lordosis and anterior pelvic tilt**, with body-part positions specified per the source's table (Czaprowski et al., 2018, p.5-11). It is read as a pattern hypothesis, not a diagnosis.

## Why it matters

It is the canonical example of the engine's two-layer reading: a fascial-line pattern ([[bodyreading_static_posture]]) plus a functional-organisation hypothesis (gluteus maximus hypoactive, hamstrings hyperactive). It demonstrates the [[apparent_shortness_vs_structural_shortening]] rule — the hamstrings may appear shortened yet be hyperactive, not contractured.

## Source-derived model

- Body-part positions: increased lumbar lordosis, anterior pelvic tilt, hip extension tendency.
- Functional organisation (per source table): gluteus maximus hypoactive; hamstrings hyperactive (compensating); hip flexors shortened-appearing.
- Corrective logic: regain gluteus maximus activity, reduce hamstring overactivity, in internal range of motion.

## Joint involvement

[[lumbar_spine]] (hyperlordosis), pelvis (anterior tilt), [[hip_joint]], [[knee_joint]], [[ankle_joint]] per the table.

## Muscle involvement

Per the source's lordotic table (p.5-11): gluteus maximus (hypoactive), hamstrings (hyperactive), hip flexors (shortened-appearing), abdominals (lengthened-appearing). These are functional-organisation hypotheses, not measured states.

## Movement or phase relationships

A child of [[non_structural_sagittal_posture]] (A); expressed via [[stabilizer_mobilizer_classification]] (A) and [[muscle_hypoactivity]]/[[muscle_hyperactivity]] (A).

## Possible myofascial relationships

Cross-linking the lordotic silhouette to a fascial line (e.g., a shortened-appearing Superficial Front Line or lengthened Deep Back Line) is an **engine synthesis (C)** from Anatomy Trains, not a claim from this source. Label it as a hypothesis.

## What a 2D app can observe

- Sagittal silhouette: lumbar curve depth, pelvic tilt, hip/knee positions — sufficient to propose the lordotic type as a hypothesis.

## What the app must not infer

- That gluteus maximus is hypoactive or hamstrings shortened from a silhouette alone (no EMG/length test).
- Diagnosis, pain source, or treatment without clinical assessment.
- Structural vs non-structural without flexibility testing.
