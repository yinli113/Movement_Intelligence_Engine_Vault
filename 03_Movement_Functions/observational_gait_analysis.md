# Observational Gait Analysis

Gait analysis by visual observation (with or without video). It is the gait-domain anchor of the vault's observability boundary: observation can describe but cannot determine biomechanical cause; kinetics/EMG require instrumentation. This mirrors [[golf_kinetics_observability_boundary]] for the gait domain.
