# Local Stabilizers

Deep, mono-articular muscles that control individual joint/spinal-segment position and provide segmental 3D stability. Members include multifidus, transversus abdominis, interspinalis, intertransversalis, semispinalis, posterior internal oblique, medial quadratus lumborum, central erector spinae, diaphragm, and pelvic floor. Prone to reflectory inhibition (hypoactivity) under stress, disuse, or lack of gravitational load.
