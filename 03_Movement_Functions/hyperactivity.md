# Hyperactivity (Muscle)

Reflectory excitation of a muscle in response to stress, manifesting clinically as reduced flexibility. The deficit of stabiliser activity triggers a compensatory mechanism: mobilisers overtake the stabilising function, producing hyperactivity and decreased flexibility — **not** the same as true muscle-fibre shortening/contracture. Corrective logic therefore targets reducing mobiliser activity and regaining stabiliser activity, not naive stretching of the apparently short muscle.
