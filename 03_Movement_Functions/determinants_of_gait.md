# Determinants of Gait

Six movements (Saunders et al.) that smooth the COM pathway: pelvic rotation, pelvic list/obliquity, knee flexion in stance, foot/ankle motion, lateral pelvic displacement, axial lower-limb rotations. Loss of two or more produces uncompensated, inefficient gait. Perry's four prerequisites plus Gage's fifth (energy conservation) define normal gait.
