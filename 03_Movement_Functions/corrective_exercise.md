# Corrective Exercise

Exercise targeting the restoration of stabiliser activity and reduction of mobiliser overactivity, performed in internal (not full) range of motion. Per Czaprowski, lengthened muscles should be **shortened** (not simply strengthened) and shortening does not equal contracture, so corrective logic targets functional organisation, not naive stretch/strengthen of the apparently short/long muscle.
