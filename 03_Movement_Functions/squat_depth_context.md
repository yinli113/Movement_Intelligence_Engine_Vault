# Squat Depth Context

## Source-grounded concept

[[straub_powers_squat_biomechanics_2024]], “Squat Depth”: shallow 0–90°, medium 90–110°, deep 110–135° knee flexion. Depth-related EMG findings conflict. Table 1 gives condition-specific prescription proposals, not diagnostic criteria.

## App translation — Level 5 engine synthesis

Specify anatomical flexion versus the internal hip–knee–ankle included angle; they are complementary under a simple segment model and must not be interchanged. Preserve the angle convention, 2D proxy label, phase and confidence. The source’s overlapping endpoints are not a ready-made scoring algorithm.

Depth is selected task context, not a universal quality target. Compare repeated trials with similar stance, load, footwear and instructions. Avoid treating a shallow trial as proof of limited hip mobility or a deep trial as evidence of superior function.

Hip/shoulder landmarks do not isolate pelvic tilt or lumbar curvature. Neither visible trunk lean nor depth establishes spinal tissue load, impingement or future injury. See [[squat_spinal_loading_correspondence_2026]] before using spinal claims from this commentary.
