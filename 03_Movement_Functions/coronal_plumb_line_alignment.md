# Coronal Plumb Line Alignment

## Definition & Biomechanical Purpose

The **Coronal Plumb Line** is a vertical line of symmetry passing through the midline of the body in the coronal (front/back) plane. It provides the reference coordinate frame for evaluating:
1. **Bilateral Symmetry**: Height differences between left and right shoulders, iliac crests, knees, and ankles.
2. **Lateral Weight Shift & Pelvic Hike**: Lateral deviation of the pelvis or torso toward one limb during standing or squatting.
3. **Stance Width Boundary Envelope**: The lateral footprint boundaries governing dynamic stability.

## Structure of the Coronal Reference System

```
       [Left Stance Rail]           [Central Midline Axis]           [Right Stance Rail]
       (Left Foot/Shoulder)         (Mid-Pelvis / Symmetrical Cleft) (Right Foot/Shoulder)
               │                                │                                │
        Dashed Red Line                   Solid Red Line                  Dashed Red Line
               │                                │                                │
        Lateral Boundary                Center of Symmetry               Lateral Boundary
```

### 1. Central Midline Solid Red Line
* **Station**: Bisects the midpoint between bilateral medial malleoli / feet, extending vertically through the pubic symphysis, umbilicus, sternum, and nasal bridge.
* **Function**: Evaluates whether the body remains centered in the gravitational midline or exhibits an unweighted pelvic shift / lateral trunk lean.

### 2. Left & Right Stance Dashed Red Rails
* **Station**: Vertical boundaries aligned with the outer edges of the feet / shoulder width.
* **Function**: Provides a visual bounding envelope to detect excessive lateral sway or pelvic drift that exceeds the base of support.
