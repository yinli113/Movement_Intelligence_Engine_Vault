# Gait Energetics

Measurement of energy expenditure during gait: expired-gas collection (metabolic cart/backpack), heart-rate method (imprecise), or mechanical work from force-plate data and COM translation. Instrumented (Level 3), not in vault. The main disadvantage of gait abnormalities is increased energy cost; energetics is an excellent outcome measure.
