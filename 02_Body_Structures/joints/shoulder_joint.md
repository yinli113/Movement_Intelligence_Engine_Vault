# Joint: Shoulder Joint

Highly mobile ball-and-socket glenohumeral joint connecting the upper limb to the scapula and thorax.

## ⚙️ Functional Role
- Provides movement and stability in its corresponding planes.
- Acts as a local hub linking muscle force to skeletal action.
