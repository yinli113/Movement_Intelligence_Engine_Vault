# Pubic Symphysis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Pubic Symphysis** resists tensile and shearing forces during single-leg stance, serving as the diagonal crossover point between [[rectus_abdominis]] and contralateral [[adductor_longus]] in the [[front_functional_line]].

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

This joint is crossed and stabilized by the following **myofascial lines**:
- [[front_functional_line]]
- [[deep_front_line]]
- [[superficial_front_line]]
