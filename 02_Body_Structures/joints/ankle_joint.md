# Joint: Ankle Joint

Hinge joint responsible for dorsiflexion and plantarflexion, adapting the foot to ground reaction forces.

## ⚙️ Functional Role
- Provides movement and stability in its corresponding planes.
- Acts as a local hub linking muscle force to skeletal action.
