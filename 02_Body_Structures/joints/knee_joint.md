# Joint: Knee Joint

Modified hinge joint allowing flexion and extension, with minor rotation when flexed. Prone to valgus collapse under lateral strain.

## ⚙️ Functional Role
- Provides movement and stability in its corresponding planes.
- Acts as a local hub linking muscle force to skeletal action.
