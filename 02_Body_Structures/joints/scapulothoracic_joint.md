# Scapulothoracic Joint

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Scapulothoracic Joint** is a muscular sliding interface providing 60 degrees of the total 180 degrees of arm elevation (2:1 scapulohumeral ratio) and supporting cross-body rotational transfer in the [[spiral_line]].

---
