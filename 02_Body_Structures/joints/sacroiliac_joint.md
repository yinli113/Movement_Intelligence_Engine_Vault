# Sacroiliac Joint

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Sacroiliac Joint** (SIJ) is the primary weight-bearing junction between the axial spine and appendicular pelvic girdle. It relies on form closure (articular topography) and dynamic force closure (tensioning from [[gluteus_maximus]], [[latissimus_dorsi]], [[sacrotuberous_ligament]], and [[thoracolumbar_fascia]]) to transmit rotational torque in gait and golf.
