# Joint: Elbow Joint

Hinge joint permitting flexion and extension of the forearm, coupled with radio-ulnar joints for pronation/supination.

## ⚙️ Functional Role
- Provides movement and stability in its corresponding planes.
- Acts as a local hub linking muscle force to skeletal action.
