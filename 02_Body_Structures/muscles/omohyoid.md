# Omohyoid

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Omohyoid** mechanically couples scapular position to anterior cervical and hyoid tension in the [[deep_front_line]].

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior belly: upper border of scapula near suprascapular notch
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Superior belly: lower border of body of hyoid bone
- **Innervation**: Ansa cervicalis (C1-C3)
