# Gastrocnemius

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Biarticular superficial calf muscle driving powerful ankle plantarflexion and knee flexion in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Medial head: popliteal surface of femur above medial condyle
  - Lateral head: lateral aspect of lateral condyle of femur
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of calcaneus via the common calcaneal (Achilles) tendon
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve (S1-S2)
