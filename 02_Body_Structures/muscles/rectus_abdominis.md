# Rectus Abdominis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Segmented anterior abdominal wall muscle flexing the lumbar spine and bracing the anterior torso in the Superficial Front Line and Front Functional Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Pubic crest and pubic symphysis
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Xiphoid process and costal cartilages of ribs 5-7
- **Innervation**: Thoracoabdominal nerves (T7-T11) and subcostal nerve (T12)
