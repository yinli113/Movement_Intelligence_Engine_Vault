# Stylohyoid

## Overview
`Stylohyoid` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:13341`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA13341`).

- **Origin**: Styloid process of temporal bone
- **Insertion**: Body of hyoid bone
- **Innervation**: Facial nerve (CN VII)
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_front_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[temporomandibular_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: elevates hyoid
- **Fascial Transmission**: Suprahyoid anchor of cranial fascial cylinder.
