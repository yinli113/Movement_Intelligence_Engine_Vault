# Extensor Digitorum

## Overview
`Extensor Digitorum` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:38500`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA38500`).

- **Origin**: Lateral epicondyle of humerus via common extensor tendon
- **Insertion**: Extensor expansions of digits 2-5 (middle and distal phalanges)
- **Innervation**: Posterior interosseous nerve (branch of Radial nerve, C7, C8)
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_back_arm_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[wrist_joint]], [[elbow_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: [[wrist_extension]], extension of MCP, PIP, and DIP joints 2-5
- **Fascial Transmission**: Primary extensor of digits 2-5 and wrist stabilizer along deep back arm line.
