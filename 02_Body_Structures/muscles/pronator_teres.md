# Pronator Teres

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Superficial anterior forearm muscle providing rapid forearm pronation and elbow flexion in the Superficial Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Humeral head: medial epicondyle; Ulnar head: coronoid process of ulna
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Middle of lateral surface of shaft of radius (pronator tuberosity)
- **Innervation**: Median nerve (C6-C7)
