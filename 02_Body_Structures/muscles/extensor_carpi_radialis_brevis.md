# Extensor Carpi Radialis Brevis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Extensor Carpi Radialis Brevis** lies medial and deep to ECRL. It is the primary pure wrist extensor, essential for stabilizing the wrist during powerful gripping.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Common extensor tendon from lateral epicondyle of humerus.
  - Radial collateral ligament.
- **Insertion**:
  - Dorsal base of the 3rd metacarpal bone.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[wrist_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: Pure wrist extension.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration
