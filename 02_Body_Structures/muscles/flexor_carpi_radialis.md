# Flexor Carpi Radialis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Flexor Carpi Radialis** is a slender fusiform muscle on the anterior forearm. In Anatomy Trains, it is part of the [[superficial_front_arm_line]] (SFAL).

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Medial epicondyle of the humerus via the common flexor tendon.
- **Insertion**:
  - Palmar surface of the base of the 2nd and 3rd metacarpal bones.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[wrist_joint]], [[elbow_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: Wrist flexion and radial deviation.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_front_arm_line]] (SFAL)**: Links medial epicondyle / intermuscular septum to the palm.
