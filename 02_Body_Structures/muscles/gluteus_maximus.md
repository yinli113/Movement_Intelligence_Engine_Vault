# Gluteus Maximus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Largest and most powerful extensor of the hip, central diagonal cross-body motor in the Back Functional Line and Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior gluteal line of ilium, posterior sacrum/coccyx, sacrotuberous ligament, and thoracolumbar fascia
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Iliotibial tract of fascia lata (upper/superficial fibers) and gluteal tuberosity of femur (lower deep fibers)
- **Innervation**: Inferior gluteal nerve (L5-S2)
