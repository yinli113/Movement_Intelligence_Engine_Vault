# Suboccipital Muscles

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Suboccipital Muscles** function primarily as high-precision craniocervical proprioceptors. They coordinate eye-head reflex coupling and serve as the cranial pivot of the [[superficial_back_line]] (SBL).

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Spinous process of axis (C2) and posterior tubercle / transverse process of atlas (C1)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior nuchal line of occiput and transverse process of atlas
- **Innervation**: Suboccipital nerve (posterior ramus of C1)
