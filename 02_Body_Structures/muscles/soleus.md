# Soleus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Broad, flat monoarticular postural calf muscle providing continuous postural plantarflexion tone against forward gravitational fall in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Soleal line of tibia, posterior head/proximal shaft of fibula, and tendinous arch
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of calcaneus via calcaneal (Achilles) tendon
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve (S1-S2)
