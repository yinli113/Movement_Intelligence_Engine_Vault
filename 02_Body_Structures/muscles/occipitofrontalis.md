# Occipitofrontalis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Cranial epicranial muscle complex tensioning the galea aponeurotica across the cranium in the Superficial Back Line and Superficial Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Frontal belly: epicranial aponeurosis; Occipital belly: lateral two-thirds of superior nuchal line of occiput
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Frontal belly: skin and subcutaneous tissue of eyebrows/forehead; Occipital belly: epicranial aponeurosis
- **Innervation**: Facial nerve (CN VII - temporal and posterior auricular branches)
