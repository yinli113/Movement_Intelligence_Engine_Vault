# Sartorius

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Longest strap muscle in the human body, crossing hip and knee to produce simultaneous flexion, abduction, and external rotation in the Ipsilateral Functional Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS) and upper half of notch below ASIS
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Superior medial surface of proximal tibia (pes anserinus)
- **Innervation**: Femoral nerve (L2-L3)
