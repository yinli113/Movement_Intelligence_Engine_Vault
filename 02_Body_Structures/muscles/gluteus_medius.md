# Gluteus Medius

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Gluteus Medius** is a fan-shaped muscle located on the posterolateral aspect of the pelvis, lying largely deep to the gluteal fascia and partly beneath [[gluteus_maximus]]. It is the principal coronal-plane pelvic stabilizer during unilateral weight-bearing and a foundational muscular component of the Anatomy Trains [[lateral_line]] (LL).

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Outer (gluteal) surface of the ilium between the anterior and posterior gluteal lines.
  - External lip of the iliac crest.
  - Gluteal aponeurosis covering its anterior two-thirds.
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Oblique ridge on the lateral and posterosuperior surface of the greater trochanter of the femur.
  - Blends intimately with the underlying tendon of [[gluteus_minimus]] and adjacent expansions of the [[iliotibial_tract]].

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Functional Anatomy

### Fiber-Specific Kinematics
- **Anterior Fibers**:
  - [[hip_abduction]], [[hip_internal_rotation]], and weak assistance in [[hip_flexion]].
  - Stabilize the femoral head in the acetabulum during early stance.
- **Posterior Fibers**:
  - [[hip_abduction]], [[hip_external_rotation]], and weak assistance in [[hip_extension]].
  - Active during terminal stance and hip loading.
- **Whole Muscle Function**:
  - **Frontal Plane Pelvic Leveling**: Contracts isometrically and eccentrically during single-leg support to prevent the contralateral pelvis from dropping (preventing the *Trendelenburg sign*).

## ⛳ Movement & Sport Specifics

- **Golf Swing Mechanics**:
  - **Trail Hip Loading (Backswing)**: The trail gluteus medius posterior fibers load eccentrically to stabilize the pelvis over the trail leg and prevent excessive lateral hip sway.
  - **Lead Hip Clearing & Impact**: The lead gluteus medius anterior fibers fire powerfully to assist lead hip internal rotation and abduction, anchoring the lead hip post as rotational torque peaks in [[max_unweighting_to_impact]].
- **Gait & Squat Dynamics**:
  - Dominant dynamic stabilizer during [[loading_response]] and single-limb stance.
  - Underactivity allows excessive femoral adduction and internal rotation, contributing to functional knee valgus and dynamic foot pronation.
