# Splenius Capitis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Broad strap muscle on the back of the neck rotating and extending the head, linking upper torso to the skull in the Spiral Line and Lateral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lower half of nuchal ligament and spinous processes of C7-T3/T4
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Mastoid process of temporal bone and rough surface of occipital bone below superior nuchal line
- **Innervation**: Posterior rami of middle cervical spinal nerves (C3-C5)
