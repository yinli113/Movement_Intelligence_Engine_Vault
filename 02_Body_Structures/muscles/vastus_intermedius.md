# Vastus Intermedius

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Vastus Intermedius** lies directly on the anterior and lateral surfaces of the femoral shaft, deep to [[rectus_femoris]] and between [[vastus_lateralis]] and [[vastus_medialis]]. It provides direct, pure sagittal-plane knee extension force.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior and lateral aspects of the proximal two-thirds of the femur.
  - Distal lateral lip of the linea aspera and lateral intermuscular septum.
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Deep lamina of the quadriceps tendon attaching to the base and superior margin of the patella.
  - Indirectly to the tibial tuberosity via the patellar ligament.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[knee_joint]] (monoarticular).
- **Primary Actions**: [[knee_extension]] across the entire arc of motion.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_front_line]] (SFL)**: Forms the deep core layer of the anterior thigh extensor track.
