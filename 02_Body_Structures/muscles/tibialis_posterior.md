# Tibialis Posterior

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deepest posterior crural muscle serving as the master dynamic dynamic stabilizer of the medial longitudinal foot arch and Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior interosseous membrane, lateral posterior tibia below soleal line, and upper two-thirds of medial fibula
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Tuberosity of navicular bone, plantar surfaces of all three cuneiforms, cuboid, and bases of metatarsals 2-4
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve (L4-L5)
