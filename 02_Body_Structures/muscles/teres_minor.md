# Teres Minor

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Posterior rotator cuff muscle providing external rotation and dynamic glenohumeral stabilization in the Deep Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Upper two-thirds of dorsal surface of lateral border of scapula
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior facet of greater tubercle of humerus and posterior shoulder capsule
- **Innervation**: Axillary nerve (C5-C6)
