# Flexor Digitorum Profundus

## Overview
`Flexor Digitorum Profundus` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:38469`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA38469`).

- **Origin**: Anteromedial surface of ulna and interosseous membrane
- **Insertion**: Distal phalanges of digits 2-5
- **Innervation**: Medial: Ulnar (C8, T1); Lateral: Anterior interosseous (C8, T1)
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_front_arm_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[wrist_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: [[wrist_flexion]]
- **Fascial Transmission**: Sole flexor of the DIP joints; essential for gripping.
