# Flexor Carpi Ulnaris

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Flexor Carpi Ulnaris** is the most medial superficial flexor of the forearm, flexing the wrist and performing ulnar deviation.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Humeral head: common flexor tendon from medial epicondyle.
  - Ulnar head: medial margin of olecranon and posterior border of ulna.
- **Insertion**:
  - Pisiform bone, and via pisohamate/pisometacarpal ligaments to hook of hamate and base of 5th metacarpal.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[wrist_joint]], [[elbow_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: Wrist flexion and ulnar deviation.
