# Quadratus Plantae

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Quadratus Plantae** rectifies the line of pull of FDL, crucial for maintaining balanced plantar grip in stance.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Medial and lateral plantar surfaces of calcaneus
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterolateral margin of tendon of flexor digitorum longus
- **Innervation**: Lateral plantar nerve (S1-S2)
