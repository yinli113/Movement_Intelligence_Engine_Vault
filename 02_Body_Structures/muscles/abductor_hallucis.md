# Abductor Hallucis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Abductor Hallucis** forms the medial border of the foot, acting as the primary dynamic bowstring of the medial longitudinal arch.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Medial process of calcaneal tuberosity, flexor retinaculum, and plantar aponeurosis
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial side of base of proximal phalanx of great toe (with medial sesamoid)
- **Innervation**: Medial plantar nerve (S1-S2)
