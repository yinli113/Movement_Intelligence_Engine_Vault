# Coracobrachialis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Coracobrachialis** is an elongated muscle situated in the upper medial part of the arm. In Anatomy Trains, it is the pivotal bridge of the [[deep_front_arm_line]] (DFAL), continuing the anterior trunk sling from [[pectoralis_minor]] onto the medial humerus and into the medial intermuscular septum.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Apex of the coracoid process of the scapula, sharing a conjoined tendon with the short head of [[biceps_brachii]].
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial border and middle third of the shaft of the humerus.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[shoulder_joint]] (glenohumeral).
- **Primary Actions**:
  - Glenohumeral flexion and [[shoulder_adduction]].
  - Stabilizes the humeral head against inferior subluxation during carrying loads.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[deep_front_arm_line]] (DFAL)**: Myers describes the direct mechanical track: ribs 3-5 -> [[pectoralis_minor]] -> coracoid process -> **[[coracobrachialis]]** / [[biceps_brachii]] -> radius and thenar eminence.
