# Pectoralis Major

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Large fan-shaped chest muscle generating powerful adduction and internal rotation, central motor of the Front Functional Line and Superficial Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Clavicular head: anterior medial clavicle; Sternocostal head: sternum, upper six costal cartilages, and external oblique aponeurosis
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral lip of intertubercular (bicipital) groove of humerus
- **Innervation**: Lateral and medial pectoral nerves (C5-T1)
