# Adductor Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Large fan-shaped medial thigh muscle serving as the pelvic anchor of the Front Functional Line and lower limb track of the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior surface of the body of the pubis, inferior to the pubic crest
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Middle third of the medial lip of the linea aspera of the femur
- **Innervation**: Obturator nerve, anterior branch (L2-L4)
