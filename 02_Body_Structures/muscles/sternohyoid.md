# Sternohyoid

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Sternohyoid** is a key anterior visceral neck stabilizer in the [[deep_front_line]], anchoring the hyoid bone downward to the thoracic cage.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of manubrium sterni and medial end of clavicle
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior border of body of hyoid bone
- **Innervation**: Ansa cervicalis (C1-C3)
