# Flexor Digitorum Superficialis

## Overview
`Flexor Digitorum Superficialis` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:38465`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA38465`).

- **Origin**: Medial epicondyle of humerus, coronoid process of ulna, and anterior radius
- **Insertion**: Middle phalanges of digits 2-5
- **Innervation**: Median nerve (C7, C8, T1)
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_front_arm_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[elbow_joint]], [[wrist_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: [[wrist_flexion]]
- **Fascial Transmission**: Primary flexor of middle phalanges and assists in wrist flexion.
