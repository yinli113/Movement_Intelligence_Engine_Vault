# Flexor Digitorum Brevis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

First-layer plantar intrinsic muscle tensioning the longitudinal arch at the ground interface of the Superficial Back Line.

- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Middle phalanges of lateral four digits (split tendons for FDL)
