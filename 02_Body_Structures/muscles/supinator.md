# Supinator

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deep spiral forearm muscle wrapping around the upper radius to produce pure forearm supination in the Deep Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral epicondyle of humerus, radial collateral ligament, annular ligament, and supinator crest of ulna
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral, posterior, and anterior surfaces of proximal third of radius
- **Innervation**: Deep branch of radial nerve / posterior interosseous nerve (C7-C8)
