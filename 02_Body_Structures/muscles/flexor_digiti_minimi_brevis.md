# Flexor Digiti Minimi Brevis (Foot)

## Overview
`Flexor Digiti Minimi Brevis (Foot)` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:45873`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA45873`).

- **Origin**: Base of 5th metatarsal
- **Insertion**: Proximal phalanx of 5th toe
- **Innervation**: Lateral plantar nerve (S2-S3)
- **Fascial Lines**: [[lateral_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[ankle_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: flexion of 5th MTP joint
- **Fascial Transmission**: Lateral plantar stabilizer supporting lateral arch.
