# Longissimus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Intermediate column of the erector spinae group maintaining vertical axial posture from pelvis to mastoid in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Common erector tendon from posterior sacrum, lumbar spinous processes, and thoracic transverse processes
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Transverse processes of thoracic/cervical vertebrae, ribs between tubercles/angles, and mastoid process of temporal bone
- **Innervation**: Posterior rami of spinal nerves (C1-L5)
