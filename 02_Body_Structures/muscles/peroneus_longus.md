# Peroneus Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Long lateral leg muscle crossing the plantar sole to form the transverse arch stirrup with tibialis anterior in the Lateral Line and Spiral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Head and proximal two-thirds of lateral surface of fibula and adjacent intermuscular septa
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Plantar lateral side of medial cuneiform and base of first metatarsal bone
- **Innervation**: Superficial fibular (peroneal) nerve (L5-S1)
