# Semimembranosus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deep medial hamstring muscle flexing and internally rotating the knee and extending the hip in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Upper outer aspect of ischial tuberosity
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of medial tibial condyle, popliteal fascia, and oblique popliteal ligament
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve division of sciatic nerve (L5-S2)
