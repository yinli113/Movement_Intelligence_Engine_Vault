# Semitendinosus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Fusiform medial hamstring muscle with a long cord-like tendon, driving hip extension and knee flexion in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Ischial tuberosity (common tendon with biceps femoris long head)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Superior medial surface of proximal tibia (pes anserinus)
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve division of sciatic nerve (L5-S2)
