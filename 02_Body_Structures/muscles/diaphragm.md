# Diaphragm

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Primary dome-shaped inspiratory muscle dividing thoracic and abdominal cavities, serving as the central dynamic respiration and core pressure anchor in the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Xiphoid process, lower six costal cartilages/ribs, arcuate ligaments, and lumbar vertebrae (crura L1-L3)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Central tendon of the diaphragm (aponeurotic sheet)
- **Innervation**: Phrenic nerve (C3-C5)
