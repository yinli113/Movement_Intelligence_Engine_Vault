# Supraspinatus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Rotator cuff muscle initiating glenohumeral abduction (0-15 degrees) and compressing the humeral head into the glenoid cavity in the Deep Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Supraspinous fossa of dorsal surface of scapula and supraspinatus fascia
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Superior facet of greater tubercle of humerus and glenohumeral joint capsule
- **Innervation**: Suprascapular nerve (C5-C6)
