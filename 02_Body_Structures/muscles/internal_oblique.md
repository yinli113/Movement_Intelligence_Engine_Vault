# Internal Oblique

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Intermediate abdominal muscle layer powering ipsilateral torso rotation and lateral flexion in the Spiral Line and Lateral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral two-thirds of inguinal ligament, anterior two-thirds of intermediate lip of iliac crest, and thoracolumbar fascia
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior borders of ribs 10-12, linea alba, and pubic crest via conjoint tendon
- **Innervation**: Thoracoabdominal nerves (T7-T11), subcostal (T12), and ilioinguinal/iliohypogastric (L1)
