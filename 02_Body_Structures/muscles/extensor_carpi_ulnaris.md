# Extensor Carpi Ulnaris

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Extensor Carpi Ulnaris** occupies the ulnar border of the posterior forearm. It extends the wrist and performs ulnar deviation, crucial in golf impact and club release.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Common extensor tendon from lateral epicondyle.
  - Posterior border of the shaft of ulna.
- **Insertion**:
  - Base of the 5th metacarpal (medial tubercle).

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[wrist_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: Wrist extension and ulnar deviation.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_back_arm_line]] (SBAL) / [[deep_back_arm_line]]**: Forms the ulnar dorsal anchor.
