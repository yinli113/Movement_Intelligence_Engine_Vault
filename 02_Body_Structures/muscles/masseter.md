# Masseter

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Primary muscle of mastication providing powerful jaw elevation, serving as the superior cranial terminus of the Deep Front Line.

- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:

- **Innervation**: Masseteric nerve from mandibular division of trigeminal nerve (CN V3)
