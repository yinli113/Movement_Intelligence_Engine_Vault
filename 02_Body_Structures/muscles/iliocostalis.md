# Iliocostalis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Most lateral column of the erector spinae group providing trunk extension, lateral flexion, and postural bracing in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Thoracolumbar fascia, posterior iliac crest, sacrum, and lower rib angles
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Angles of all ribs and transverse processes of cervical vertebrae (C4-C6)
- **Innervation**: Posterior rami of spinal nerves (C4-L3)
