# Pectineus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Flat quadrangular transitional muscle between hip flexors and adductors, contributing to Deep Front Line pelvic balance.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Pectineal line of the pubis (pecten pubis)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Pectineal line of the femur (inferior to lesser trochanter)
- **Innervation**: Femoral nerve (L2-L3) and occasionally obturator nerve
