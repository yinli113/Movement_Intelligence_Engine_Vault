# Biceps Brachii

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Two-headed anterior arm muscle acting as the workhorse supinator of the forearm and powerful elbow flexor in the Deep Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Short head: apex of coracoid process of scapula
  - Long head: supraglenoid tubercle of scapula and glenoid labrum
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Radial tuberosity and bicipital aponeurosis into deep fascia of medial forearm
- **Innervation**: Musculocutaneous nerve (C5-C6)
