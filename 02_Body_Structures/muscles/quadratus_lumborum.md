# Quadratus Lumborum

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Posterior abdominal wall muscle stabilizing the 12th rib, controlling lateral trunk flexion, and acting in the Deep Front Line and Lateral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Internal lip of iliac crest and iliolumbar ligament
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior border of rib 12 and transverse processes of L1-L4 vertebrae
- **Innervation**: Subcostal nerve (T12) and anterior rami of lumbar spinal nerves (L1-L4)
