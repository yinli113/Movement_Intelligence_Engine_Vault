# Infraspinatus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Posterior rotator cuff muscle driving primary external rotation and posterior humeral head stability in the Deep Back Arm Line.

- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Middle facet of greater tubercle of humerus and glenohumeral joint capsule
