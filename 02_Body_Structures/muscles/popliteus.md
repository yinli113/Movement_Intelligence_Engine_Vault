# Popliteus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deep posterior knee muscle unlocking the knee joint by externally rotating the femur (or internally rotating the tibia) in the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral condyle of femur (intracapsular popliteal groove) and posterior horn of lateral meniscus
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of proximal tibia superior to soleal line
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve (L4-S1)
