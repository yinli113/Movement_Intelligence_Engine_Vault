# Flexor Digitorum Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deep posterior compartment leg muscle providing plantar toe grip, longitudinal arch maintenance, and Deep Front Line floor support.

---

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of tibia inferior to soleal line and intermuscular septum
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Bases of distal phalanges of lateral four toes (plantar aspect)
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve (L5-S2)
