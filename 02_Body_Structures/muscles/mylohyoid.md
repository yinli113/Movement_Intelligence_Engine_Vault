# Mylohyoid

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Mylohyoid** forms the muscular sling under the tongue, connecting the mandible to the hyoid in the [[deep_front_line]].

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Mylohyoid line of inner surface of mandible
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Body of hyoid bone and median fibrous raphe
- **Innervation**: Mylohyoid nerve from inferior alveolar branch of CN V3
