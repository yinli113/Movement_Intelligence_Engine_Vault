# Extensor Digitorum Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Anterior crural muscle extending the lateral four digits and dorsiflexing the ankle, key distal component of the Superficial Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral condyle of tibia, proximal three-fourths of anterior surface of fibula, and interosseous membrane
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Dorsal digital expansions into bases of middle and distal phalanges of lateral four toes
- **Innervation**: Deep fibular (peroneal) nerve (L5-S1)
