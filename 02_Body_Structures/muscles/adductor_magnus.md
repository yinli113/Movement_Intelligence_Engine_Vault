# Adductor Magnus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Massive triangular medial thigh muscle functioning as the primary coronal plane stabilizer of the hip and primary bridge of the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior pubic ramus, ischial ramus (adductor part), and ischial tuberosity (hamstring part)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Gluteal tuberosity, linea aspera, medial supracondylar line, and adductor tubercle of femur
- **Innervation**: Obturator nerve posterior branch (L2-L4) for adductor part; Tibial nerve (L4) for hamstring part
