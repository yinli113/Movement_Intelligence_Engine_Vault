# Subclavius

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Small cylindrical muscle stabilizing and depressing the clavicle during shoulder girdle movement in the Deep Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Junction of first rib and its costal cartilage
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Subclavian groove on the inferior surface of the middle third of clavicle
- **Innervation**: Nerve to subclavius (C5-C6)
