# Tensor Fasciae Latae

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Anterolateral hip muscle tensioning the iliotibial tract to stabilize the pelvis and lateral knee in the Lateral Line and Spiral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior outer lip of iliac crest, anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS), and notch below ASIS
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Iliotibial tract of fascia lata at the junction of upper and middle thirds of thigh
- **Innervation**: Superior gluteal nerve (L4-S1)
