# Biceps Femoris (Long Head)

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Lateral hamstring muscle linking the ischial tuberosity and sacrotuberous ligament to the fibular head in the Superficial Back Line and Spiral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Ischial tuberosity (common tendon with semitendinosus) and sacrotuberous ligament
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral side of the head of the fibula and lateral tibial condyle
- **Innervation**: Tibial division of sciatic nerve (L5-S2)
