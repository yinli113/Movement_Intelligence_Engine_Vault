# Rectus Capitis Lateralis

## Overview
`Rectus Capitis Lateralis` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:71302`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA71302`).

- **Origin**: Transverse process of atlas (C1)
- **Insertion**: Jugular process of occipital bone
- **Innervation**: Anterior ramus of C1
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_front_line]], [[lateral_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: Intrinsic cranial / axial stability

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: lateral flexion of head
- **Fascial Transmission**: Deep lateral suboccipital stabilizer.
