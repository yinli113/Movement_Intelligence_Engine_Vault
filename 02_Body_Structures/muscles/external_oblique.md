# External Oblique

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Superficial lateral abdominal muscle driving contralateral trunk rotation and lateral flexion, linking the rib cage to the pelvis in the Spiral Line and Functional Lines.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - External surfaces of lower eight ribs (ribs 5-12)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Linea alba, pubic crest, pubic tubercle, and anterior half of outer lip of iliac crest
- **Innervation**: Thoracoabdominal nerves (T7-T11) and subcostal nerve (T12)
