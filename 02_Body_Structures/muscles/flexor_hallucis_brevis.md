# Flexor Hallucis Brevis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Flexor Hallucis Brevis** provides the critical terminal push-off stability under the first MTP joint in the [[deep_front_line]].

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Plantar surfaces of cuboid and lateral cuneiform, and tendon of tibialis posterior
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial and lateral sides of base of proximal phalanx of great toe (via sesamoids)
- **Innervation**: Medial plantar nerve (S1-S2)
