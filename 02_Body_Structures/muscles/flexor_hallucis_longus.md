# Flexor Hallucis Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Powerful deep posterior flexor providing the final propulsion push-off in gait and primary lower extremity anchor of the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Distal two-thirds of posterior surface of fibula and interosseous membrane
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Plantar surface of the base of the distal phalanx of the great toe
- **Innervation**: Tibial nerve (S1-S2)
