# Gemellus Inferior

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Gemellus Inferior** arises from the ischial tuberosity and joins the inferior border of the [[obturator_internus]] tendon to insert on the greater trochanter.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Upper part of the tuberosity of the ischium.
- **Insertion**:
  - Medial surface of greater trochanter.
