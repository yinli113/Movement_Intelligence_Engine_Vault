# Thyrohyoid

## Overview
`Thyrohyoid` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:13345`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA13345`).

- **Origin**: Oblique line of thyroid cartilage
- **Insertion**: Greater horn and body of hyoid bone
- **Innervation**: C1 via CN XII
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_front_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[temporomandibular_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: depresses hyoid, elevates larynx
- **Fascial Transmission**: Fascial bridge between thyroid cartilage and hyoid bone.
