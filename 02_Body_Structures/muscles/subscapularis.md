# Subscapularis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Largest rotator cuff muscle providing powerful internal rotation and dynamic anterior humeral head stabilization in the Deep Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Medial two-thirds of subscapular fossa of anterior scapula
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lesser tubercle of humerus and anterior capsule of glenohumeral joint
- **Innervation**: Upper and lower subscapular nerves (C5-C6)
