# Adductor Brevis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Short adductor muscle of the medial thigh, acting in hip adduction, flexion, and medial rotation as part of the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - External surface of the body and inferior pubic ramus
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Pectineal line and proximal part of linea aspera of femur
- **Innervation**: Obturator nerve (L2-L4)
