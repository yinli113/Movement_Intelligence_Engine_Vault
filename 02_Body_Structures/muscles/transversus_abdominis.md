# Transversus Abdominis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deepest abdominal muscle forming the muscular corset of the core cylinder, essential for intra-abdominal pressure generation in the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral third of inguinal ligament, anterior two-thirds of internal lip of iliac crest, thoracolumbar fascia, and lower six costal cartilages
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Linea alba, pubic crest, and pectineal line of pubis via conjoint tendon
- **Innervation**: Thoracoabdominal nerves (T7-T11), subcostal (T12), iliohypogastric and ilioinguinal nerves (L1)
