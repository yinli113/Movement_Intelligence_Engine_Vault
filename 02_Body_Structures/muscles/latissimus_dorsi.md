# Latissimus Dorsi

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Broadest back muscle connecting torso and pelvic fascia to the upper limb, driving upper power in the Back Functional Line and Superficial Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Spinous processes of T7-L5, thoracolumbar fascia, posterior third of iliac crest, lower 3-4 ribs, and inferior angle of scapula
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Floor of intertubercular (bicipital) groove of humerus
- **Innervation**: Thoracodorsal nerve (C6-C8)
