# Semispinalis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Semispinalis** is a deep posterior muscle group spanning the thoracic, cervical, and capitis regions. It is a powerful extensor and stabilizer of the head and neck.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Transverse processes of C4–T12 vertebrae.
- **Insertion**:
  - Spinous processes of C2–T4 and between superior and inferior nuchal lines of occiput.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[cervical_spine]], [[thoracic_spine]].
- **Primary Actions**: Head/neck extension and contralateral rotation.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_back_line]] (SBL) / [[deep_front_line]]**: Anchors the upper axial column to the occipital ridge.
