# Iliacus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Fan-shaped pelvic basin muscle acting with psoas major as the prime flexor of the hip joint in the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Superior two-thirds of iliac fossa, internal lip of iliac crest, and lateral sacrum
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lesser trochanter of femur (via conjoined tendon with psoas major)
- **Innervation**: Femoral nerve (L2-L4)
