# Gluteus Minimus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deepest gluteal muscle stabilizing the femoral head within the acetabulum and driving internal rotation in the Lateral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Outer surface of ilium between anterior and inferior gluteal lines
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior border of greater trochanter of femur
- **Innervation**: Superior gluteal nerve (L4-S1)
