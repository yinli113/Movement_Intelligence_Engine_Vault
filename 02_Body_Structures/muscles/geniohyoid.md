# Geniohyoid

## Overview
`Geniohyoid` is an anatomical muscle indexed in the Foundational Model of Anatomy (`FMA:46320`) and BodyParts3D (`FMA46320`).

- **Origin**: Inferior mental spine of mandible
- **Insertion**: Anterior surface of hyoid bone
- **Innervation**: C1 via CN XII
- **Fascial Lines**: [[deep_front_line]]
- **Joints Crossed**: [[temporomandibular_joint]]

## Functional & Movement Role
- **Primary Actions**: draws hyoid anterosuperiorly
- **Fascial Transmission**: Flooring suprahyoid muscle along deep front line.
