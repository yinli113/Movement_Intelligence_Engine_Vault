# Brachioradialis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Brachioradialis** forms the lateral boundary of the cubital fossa and the prominent lateral contour of the proximal forearm. It flexes the elbow most effectively when the forearm is in a semi-pronated (neutral) position.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Upper two-thirds of the lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus.
  - Lateral intermuscular septum.
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral base of the radial styloid process at the distal radius.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[elbow_joint]], [[wrist_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**:
  - [[elbow_flexion]] (especially with neutral forearm grip).
  - Semi-pronation / semi-supination returning forearm to neutral.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_back_arm_line]] (SBAL) & [[deep_front_arm_line]]**: Forms the transitional radial bridge between the lateral humerus and the radial styloid.
