# Temporalis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Fan-shaped muscle of mastication elevating and retracting the mandible, terminal cranial cranial anchor in the Deep Front Line.

---

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Temporal fossa of skull and deep temporal fascia
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Coronoid process and anterior border of ramus of mandible
- **Innervation**: Deep temporal branches of mandibular nerve (CN V3)
