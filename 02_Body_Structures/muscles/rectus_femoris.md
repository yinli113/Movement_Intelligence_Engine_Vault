# Rectus Femoris

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Only biarticular head of the quadriceps femoris group, linking hip flexion to knee extension as the primary anterior thigh track of the Superficial Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Straight head: anterior inferior iliac spine (AIIS); Reflected head: superior groove of acetabulum and hip capsule
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Base of patella and via patellar ligament to tibial tuberosity
- **Innervation**: Femoral nerve (L2-L4)
