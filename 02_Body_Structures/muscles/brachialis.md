# Brachialis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Brachialis** is a powerful muscle located in the anterior compartment of the arm, deep to [[biceps_brachii]]. Because it inserts on the ulna rather than the radius, it acts as a pure [[elbow_flexion|elbow flexor]] regardless of forearm pronation or supination position.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Distal half of the anterior aspect of the shaft of the humerus.
  - Medial and lateral intermuscular septa of the arm.
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Tuberosity of the ulna and coronoid process of the ulna.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[elbow_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: Pure [[elbow_flexion]] in all forearm positions.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[deep_front_arm_line]] (DFAL)**: Sits immediately deep to the biceps brachii track, anchoring directly into the ulnar side of the forearm.
