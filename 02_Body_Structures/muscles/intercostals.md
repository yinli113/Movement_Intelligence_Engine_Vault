# Intercostals

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Thoracic wall muscle layers (external, internal, innermost) governing rib cage expansion, torsion control, and breathing mechanics in the Lateral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior borders of ribs 1-11
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Superior borders of adjacent ribs below (ribs 2-12)
- **Innervation**: Intercostal nerves (T1-T11)
