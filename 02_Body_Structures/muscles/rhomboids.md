# Rhomboids

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Diamond-shaped upper back muscles retracting and downwardly rotating the scapula, central link with serratus anterior in the Spiral Line and Deep Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Rhomboid minor: nuchal ligament and spinous processes C7-T1; Rhomboid major: spinous processes T2-T5
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial (vertebral) border of scapula from spine to inferior angle
- **Innervation**: Dorsal scapular nerve (C4-C5)
