# Gemellus Superior

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Gemellus Superior** acts in conjunction with [[obturator_internus]] and [[gemellus_inferior]] as part of the triceps coxae to stabilize the femoral head.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Ischial spine.
- **Insertion**:
  - Medial surface of greater trochanter via common obturator internus tendon.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[hip_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: [[hip_external_rotation]] and acetabular centering.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[deep_front_line]] (DFL)**: Part of the deep posterior pelvic rotator group.
