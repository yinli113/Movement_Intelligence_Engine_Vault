# Gracilis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Slender biarticular medial thigh strap muscle adducting the hip and flexing/internally rotating the knee in the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Inferior ramus of pubis and anterior margin of ischiopubic ramus
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial surface of proximal tibia (pes anserinus)
- **Innervation**: Obturator nerve (L2-L3)
