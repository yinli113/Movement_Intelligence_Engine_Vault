# Pronator Quadratus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deep quadrangular forearm muscle driving primary forearm pronation and binding distal radius and ulna.

---

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Distal anterior surface and border of shaft of ulna
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Distal anterior surface and lateral border of shaft of radius
- **Innervation**: Anterior interosseous nerve from median nerve (C8-T1)
