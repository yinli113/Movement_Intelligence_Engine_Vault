# Quadratus Femoris

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Quadratus Femoris** is a quadrilateral muscle of the posterior hip, situated below the inferior gemellus and obturator internus. It is a powerful external rotator of the thigh.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Lateral border of the tuberosity of the ischium.
- **Insertion**:
  - Quadrate tubercle and vertical line on the intertrochanteric crest of the femur.
