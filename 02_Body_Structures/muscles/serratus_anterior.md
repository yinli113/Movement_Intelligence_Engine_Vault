# Serratus Anterior

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Saw-toothed lateral thoracic muscle protracting and upwardly rotating the scapula, anchoring the scapula to the rib cage in the Spiral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Outer surfaces of upper eight or nine ribs (ribs 1-8/9)
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Costal surface of the entire medial border of scapula
- **Innervation**: Long thoracic nerve (C5-C7)
