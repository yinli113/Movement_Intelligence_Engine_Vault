# Digastric

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Digastric** bridges the skull to the hyoid bone and mandible, serving as a dynamic anterior cranial sling in the [[deep_front_line]].

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior belly: mastoid notch of temporal bone; Anterior belly: digastric fossa of mandible
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Intermediate tendon attached to hyoid bone by a fibrous sling
- **Innervation**: Anterior belly: mylohyoid nerve (CN V3); Posterior belly: facial nerve (CN VII)
