# Splenius Cervicis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Splenius Cervicis** is a strap-like muscle located on the posterior neck and upper back. It functions alongside [[splenius_capitis]] as a major postural rotator and extensor of the cervical column, anchoring the upper cervical vertebrae to the thoracic spine.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Inferior/Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Spinous processes of the 3rd to 6th thoracic vertebrae (T3-T6).
- **Insertion (Superior/Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior tubercles of the transverse processes of the upper two or three cervical vertebrae (C1-C3).

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[cervical_spine]], upper [[thoracic_spine]].
- **Primary Actions**:
  - Unilateral: ipsilateral rotation and lateral flexion of the neck.
  - Bilateral: cervical extension and posterior head carriage stabilization.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[spiral_line]] (SPL)**: Works in continuity with [[splenius_capitis]], linking cross-body rotational loops from the thoracic spine and contralateral rhomboid/serratus sling up to the skull and cervical pivot.
