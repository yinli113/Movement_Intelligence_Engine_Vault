# Triceps Brachii

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Three-headed posterior arm muscle acting as the prime extensor of the elbow joint and stabilizer of the glenohumeral joint in the Deep Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Long head: infraglenoid tubercle of scapula; Lateral head: posterior humerus above radial groove; Medial head: posterior humerus below radial groove
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of olecranon process of ulna and deep fascia of forearm
- **Innervation**: Radial nerve (C6-C8)
