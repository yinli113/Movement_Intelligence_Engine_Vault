# Levator Ani

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Levator Ani** forms the primary dynamic muscular floor of the core cylinder. In Anatomy Trains, it is the bottom pelvic trampoline of the [[deep_front_line]] (DFL), working with the [[diaphragm]] and [[transversus_abdominis]] to regulate intra-abdominal pressure.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Posterior surface of body of pubis, tendinous arch of obturator fascia, and ischial spine
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Perineal body, anococcygeal ligament, walls of prostate/vagina and rectum, and coccyx
- **Innervation**: Pudendal nerve (S2-S4) and nerve to levator ani (S4)
