# Extensor Carpi Radialis Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Extensor Carpi Radialis Longus** is a key extensor and radial deviator of the wrist. In Anatomy Trains, it forms part of the [[superficial_back_arm_line]] (SBAL) continuing from the lateral humerus down to the dorsal metacarpals.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Distal one-third of the lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus.
  - Lateral intermuscular septum.
- **Insertion**:
  - Dorsal base of the 2nd metacarpal bone (radial side).

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[wrist_joint]], [[elbow_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**:
  - Wrist extension and radial deviation (abduction).
  - Weak elbow flexion assistance.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_back_arm_line]] (SBAL)**: Extends the dorsal arm track from the lateral epicondyle / intermuscular septum to the fingers.
