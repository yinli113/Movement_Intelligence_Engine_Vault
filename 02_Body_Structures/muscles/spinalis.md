# Spinalis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Most medial column of the erector spinae group linking spinous processes to maintain sagittal spinal extension in the Superficial Back Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Spinous processes of upper lumbar and lower thoracic vertebrae
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Spinous processes of upper thoracic and cervical vertebrae
- **Innervation**: Posterior rami of spinal nerves
