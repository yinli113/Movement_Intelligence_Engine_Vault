# Psoas Major

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Primary deep central core motor linking the axial lumbar spine directly to the lower extremity, central pillar of the Deep Front Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Bodies and transverse processes of T12-L5 vertebrae and intervening intervertebral discs
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lesser trochanter of femur (via conjoined tendon with iliacus)
- **Innervation**: Anterior rami of lumbar spinal nerves (L1-L3)
