# Obturator Externus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Obturator Externus** is a flat, triangular muscle covering the outer surface of the anterior pelvic wall. It anchors the femoral neck and head tightly into the acetabulum.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - External surface of the obturator membrane and adjacent pubic/ischial rami.
- **Insertion**:
  - Trochanteric fossa of the femur.
