# Tibialis Anterior

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Prime dorsiflexor and inverter of the ankle, creating the medial transverse arch stirrup in the Superficial Front Line and Spiral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral condyle and proximal half to two-thirds of lateral surface of tibia, and interosseous membrane
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial and plantar surfaces of medial cuneiform and base of first metatarsal bone
- **Innervation**: Deep fibular (peroneal) nerve (L4-L5)
