# Deltoid

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Triangular shoulder muscle providing coronal plane glenohumeral abduction, anterior flexion, and posterior extension in the Superficial Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior border/lateral third of clavicle, lateral acromion, and lower lip of spine of scapula
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Deltoid tuberosity on the middle of the lateral surface of the humerus
- **Innervation**: Axillary nerve (C5-C6)
