# Extensor Hallucis Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Anterior compartment deep crural muscle extending the big toe and dorsiflexing the foot, driving toe clearance in gait.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Middle half of anterior surface of fibula and adjacent interosseous membrane
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Dorsal surface of the base of the distal phalanx of the great toe (hallux)
- **Innervation**: Deep fibular (peroneal) nerve (L5-S1)
