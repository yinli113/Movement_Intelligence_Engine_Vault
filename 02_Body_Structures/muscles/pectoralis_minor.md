# Pectoralis Minor

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Deep anterior chest muscle stabilizing and depressing the scapula, serving as the pivotal chest-to-arm bridge in the Deep Front Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Anterior surfaces and upper margins of ribs 3, 4, and 5 near costal cartilages
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Medial border and superior surface of coracoid process of scapula
- **Innervation**: Medial pectoral nerve (C8-T1)
