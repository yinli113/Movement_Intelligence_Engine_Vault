# Palmaris Longus

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Palmaris Longus** is a superficial muscle that directly tensions the palmar fascia. In Myers Anatomy Trains, it provides direct myofascial continuity to the palmar aponeurosis in the [[superficial_front_arm_line]] (SFAL).

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin**:
  - Common flexor tendon from medial epicondyle of humerus.
- **Insertion**:
  - Central part of the flexor retinaculum and apex of the palmar aponeurosis.

---

## ⚙️ Biomechanical Role & Joint Mechanics

- **Joints Acted Upon**: [[wrist_joint]].
- **Primary Actions**: Tensions palmar aponeurosis and weakly flexes wrist.

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

- **[[superficial_front_arm_line]] (SFAL)**: Direct muscular continuous anchor to the palmar fascia.
