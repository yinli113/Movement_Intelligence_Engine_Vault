# Trapezius

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Large diamond-shaped superficial back muscle governing scapular elevation, upward rotation, retraction, and depression in the Superficial Back Arm Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - External occipital protuberance, superior nuchal line, nuchal ligament, and spinous processes of C7-T12
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral third of clavicle, acromion, and upper border of spine of scapula
- **Innervation**: Accessory nerve (CN XI motor) and anterior rami of C3-C4 (proprioception)
