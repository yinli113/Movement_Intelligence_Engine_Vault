# Sternocleidomastoid

## 📖 Definition & Overview

Prominent two-headed neck muscle governing head rotation and cervical posture, superior cranial anchor of the Superficial Front Line and Lateral Line.

---

## 🦴 Bony Attachments & Anatomical Landmarks

- **Origin (Proximal Attachments)**:
  - Sternal head: anterior surface of manubrium sterni; Clavicular head: superior medial third of clavicle
- **Insertion (Distal Attachments)**:
  - Lateral surface of mastoid process of temporal bone and lateral half of superior nuchal line of occiput
- **Innervation**: Accessory nerve (CN XI) and anterior rami of C2-C3
