# Calcaneal Tendon

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Calcaneal Tendon** (Achilles tendon) is the common distal conjoined tendon of [[gastrocnemius]] and [[soleus]]. In Anatomy Trains, it is the pivotal elastic energy storage spring of the [[superficial_back_line]] (SBL), continuing continuous tension across the calcaneus into the [[plantar_fascia]].
