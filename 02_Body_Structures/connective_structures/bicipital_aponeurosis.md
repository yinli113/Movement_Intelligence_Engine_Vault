# Bicipital Aponeurosis

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Bicipital Aponeurosis** (lacertus fibrosus) mechanically bridges the biceps brachii tendon to the pronator-flexor group of the medial forearm, demonstrated by Stecco to transmit significant fascial tension across the elbow.
