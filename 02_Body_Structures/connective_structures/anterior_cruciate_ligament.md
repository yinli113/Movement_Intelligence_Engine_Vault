# Anterior Cruciate Ligament

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Anterior Cruciate Ligament** (ACL) originates from the medial aspect of the lateral femoral condyle and inserts onto the anterior intercondylar area of the tibia, controlling sagittal and rotational knee joint stability.
