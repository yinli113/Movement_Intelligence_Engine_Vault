# Linea Alba

## 📖 Definition & Overview

The **Linea Alba** is a dense, avascular collagenous band running down the anterior abdominal midline. It acts as the anchor line for cross-body torsional force transmission in the [[spiral_line]] and [[front_functional_line]].

---

## 🕸️ Myofascial Integration

This connective structure participates in the following **myofascial lines**:

- [[front_functional_line]]
- [[deep_front_line]]
