# Biomechanical Action: Hip Internal Rotation

Hip internal rotation is the femur rotating inward relative to the pelvis. In a golf knowledge graph, it is the key lead-side pivot mechanic during [[golf_swing_transition]] and early downswing.

## Golf Reasoning Role
The lead hip must accept internal rotation so the pelvis can open without sliding. When this mechanic is limited, the golfer may borrow motion from the lumbar spine, knee, foot, or trail shoulder.

## Failure Patterns
- Lead knee collapses inward rather than the hip rotating cleanly.
- Pelvis sways toward the target instead of turning.
- Lead foot rolls excessively or the big toe cannot stay loaded.
- The trunk or neck braces to create a sense of rotation.

## Reasoning Prompt
If transition sequencing breaks down, check [[toe_loading]] and lead [[hip_internal_rotation]] before interpreting the issue as purely upper-body timing.
