# Intervention: Gluteus Maximus Releases

Myofascial release (MFR) protocols to alleviate trigger points in the gluteal tissue and restore sliding capacity to the posterior and lateral compartments.

---

## 1. Lacrosse Ball Trigger Point Release
- **Objective**: Target localized trigger points (knotting) in the glute max and underlying deep rotators.
- **Execution**:
  1. Sit on a lacrosse ball (or tennis ball for less intensity), placing it under the glute cheek.
  2. Bend your knees, keeping feet flat on the floor.
  3. Cross the ankle of the target leg over the opposite knee (figure-4 position) to stretch the glute tissue and expose the deeper structures.
  4. Search for tender spots (trigger points). Once found, hold pressure on that spot for 30–60 seconds while taking deep belly breaths.
- **Precaution**: Avoid direct pressure over the sciatic nerve (if you feel sharp tingling radiating down your leg, shift the ball immediately).

## 2. Foam Roll Gluteal Shearing
- **Objective**: Restore sliding between the superficial fascia, gluteus maximus, and the deep rotators.
- **Execution**:
  1. Sit on a foam roller, tilt slightly to one side so the roller sits on one glute cheek.
  2. Roll slowly from the top of the hip (iliac crest) down to the sit bone (ischial tuberosity).
  3. Perform a "shearing" movement by slowly rocking side-to-side across the muscle fibers rather than rolling up and down.
  4. Spend 2 minutes per side.
