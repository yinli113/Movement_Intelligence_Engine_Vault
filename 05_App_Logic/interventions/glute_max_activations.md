# Intervention: Gluteus Maximus Activations

These drills are designed to isolate and wake up the Gluteus Maximus, correcting "glute amnesia" before integrating it into dynamic athletic movements (like the golf swing).

---

## 1. Iso-Bridge (Isolated Activation)
- **Objective**: Wake up the glutes without hamstring or lower back compensation.
- **Execution**:
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent at $90^\circ$, feet flat on the floor.
  2. Perform a posterior pelvic tilt (flatten your lower back to the floor).
  3. Drive through your heels to lift your hips.
  4. Squeeze your glutes hard at the top. Hold for 5 seconds.
  5. *Regression*: Single-leg bridge if double-leg is too easy, ensuring the pelvis remains level.
- **Cue**: "Pinch a coin between your cheeks. Do not let your lower back arch."

## 2. Banded Glute Kickbacks (SBL Connection)
- **Objective**: Activate the glute max in its primary extension plane.
- **Execution**:
  1. Stand with a mini-band around your ankles.
  2. Shift weight to one leg, keeping a soft bend in the knee.
  3. Kick the opposite leg back at a $45^\circ$ angle (combining extension and slight external rotation).
  4. Hold for 2 seconds, return slowly.
- **Cue**: "Drive from the hip, not by bending at the lower back."

## 3. Contralateral Sling Load (Functional Line Integration)
- **Objective**: Link the glute max to the opposite latissimus dorsi to load the Back Functional Line.
- **Execution**:
  1. Step back into a reverse lunge with your right leg.
  2. As you drive up, pull your right knee up to your chest while simultaneously driving your left elbow forward and right arm back (contralateral rotation).
  3. Repeat for 10-12 reps per side.
- **Cue**: "Feel the tension transfer diagonally from your standing glute across your back to your opposite shoulder."
